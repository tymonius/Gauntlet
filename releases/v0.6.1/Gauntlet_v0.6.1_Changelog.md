# Gauntlet v0.6.1 Changelog


## Post-release playtest changes

- **Mystics — Guardians of the Circle:** Spirit Walker may now protect either a begun Rite or the Ritual of Ascendance from the first battle-loss interruption on their turn by putting an Arcane card from Hand in the Graveyard whose value is at least 1 plus the number of completed Rites. The ability still cannot preserve a continuing position requirement.

- **Faction Action presentation:** Added a dedicated Faction Actions summary to every faction chapter, including explicit none entries where appropriate, and separated Action-Opportunity actions from other faction abilities and procedures.

- **Terminology refinement:** Distinguished Position from Occupation; defined Occupier and Counterattack; renamed deckbuilding value to card value; defined Faction Ability and Faction Action; renamed the battle step **Determine the Outcome**; and replaced the overloaded timing verb with operation-specific language.

- **Financiers — Financial Capacity:** After the Capture step and its effects, a Financier whose Treasury value exceeds their controlled Territories may use both normal Action Opportunities that turn, provided at least one is used for a Financier Faction Action. Eligibility is locked for the turn.

**Compared with:** v0.6.0 — Faction Framework Release  
**Published:** July 30, 2026

Gauntlet v0.6.1 is the first physical-playtest revision. It preserves the faction framework, card counts, Territory pool, Deck construction rules, and principal victory conditions of v0.6.0 while revising the battle procedure, rules language, timing framework, onboarding structure, faction-card text, and playtest workflow.

## Rulebook and onboarding

- Rebuilt the rulebook around two complementary layers:
  - **How it works** teaches the ordinary case in clear language.
  - **Complete rules** provides exact timing, exceptions, and edge cases.
- Added explicit recommended play areas for Draw Pile, Discard Pile, Graveyard, Hand, Asset Bank, Reserve, Gambit, Tactic, Leader, and faction components.
- Separated instruction/setup time, game time, and total session time in the playtest standard.
- Added stopped-session classifications so externally interrupted games are not treated as completed-game pacing or balance evidence.
- Added faction introductions and recommended-Deck onboarding requirements.

## Battle vocabulary and sequence

- Replaced the obsolete term **hand commitment** with **Gambit**.
- Replaced the obsolete term **Battle Hand** with **Reserve**.
- Replaced “a card chosen from the obsolete Battle Hand” with **Tactic**.
- Added dedicated **Gambit** and **Tactic** printed headings.
- Retained **Battle** as a flexible heading eligible for either role.
- Standardized **Pass** when declining to set or choose a card.
- Added the mnemonic: **Gambits go to the Graveyard.**

Battles now proceed in this order:

1. opening effects;
2. set Gambits;
3. form Reserves;
4. reveal Gambits;
5. choose Tactics;
6. reveal Tactics;
7. **Determine the Outcome**; and
8. proceed to the Aftermath.

## Reveal, priority, and replacement

- Gambits and Tactics reveal simultaneously within their respective stages.
- Effects that expressly apply before other effects at that stage are applied first.
- Added attacker-first alternating application when both players control effects at the same timing.
- Clarified that simultaneous reveal does not cause effects to be applied simultaneously.
- Clarified that a face-up Gambit or Tactic still waits for its normal reveal timing unless stated otherwise.
- Clarified that effects cannot be negated after they have been applied.
- Added shared rules for replacement cards, revised choices, multiple Tactics, and additional Tactics.
- A replacement takes the same role and does not reopen an earlier timing window.

## Aftermath, withdrawal, and retreat

- Replaced loose uses of “after battle” and the obsolete term “battle cleanup” with the formal **Aftermath of the battle**.
- Added a complete Aftermath order covering result, withdrawal replacement, retreat, changes in Occupation, additional retreat, clearing battle cards, effects triggered by that movement, and follow-up movement.
- The rules now state directly where battle cards go:
  - Gambits go to the Graveyard;
  - Tactics go to the Discard Pile; and
  - cards remaining in Reserve go to the Discard Pile.
- Defined effect-caused withdrawal separately from retreat.
- Withdrawal ends the battle without a winner or loser and does not trigger win, loss, or retreat effects.
- Clarified attacker and defender movement for withdrawal and retreat.

## Movement and Military Orders

- Clarified that movement ending in battle loses all unused movement.
- A player moves after a battle only when a rule or effect explicitly permits it.
- Revised **Onward** to occur during Movement before a battle begins.
- Revised **Rout** to occur at the end of the Aftermath of a battle the player initiated and won.
- Clarified that post-battle movement may start another battle only when the effect permits it.

## Assets and Overlays

- Added Asset replacement at capacity: discard one controlled Asset to make room and bank the new Asset during the same Action Opportunity.
- A restriction preventing an Asset from leaving play also prevents replacing it this way.
- Clarified that an Overlay is controlled by the controller of the Territory beneath it.
- Overlay control changes with Territory control; ownership does not.
- A removed Overlay normally goes to its owner's Discard Pile.
- Clarified the perspective of **you** on Overlays.

## Shared interaction rules

- Added a common copied-effect framework for Treason, Heresy, Witchcraft, Rend the Veil, Arcane Knowledge, and similar effects.
- Copied effects retain costs, requirements, targets, and timing restrictions.
- Copied effects cannot reopen earlier timing windows.
- A copied effect cannot normally select another copied-effect instruction.
- Added standard verbs for setting Gambits, choosing Tactics, playing cards, using Assets and Faction Abilities, and applying effects.

## Diplomats

- Terms now conclude during opening effects before Gambits are set.
- Accepted Terms prevent the pending battle; battle and Aftermath triggers do not occur.
- Added Diplomat mirror procedure: attacker may offer Terms first; if they pass, defender may offer. Only one Proposal may be offered for the pending battle.
- Revised Neutral Observers to set Gambit order explicitly.
- Revised Gunboat Diplomacy, Demilitarized Zone, Sanctions, and other cards for the new timing and Overlay rules.

## Intelligence

- Surveillance may be used once at the Gambit stage and once at the Tactic stage in the same battle.
- Each Tactic revealed through Surveillance costs 1 Intel.
- Interference costs 2 Intel per revealed target and may target a face-up commitment directly without Surveillance.
- A removed Gambit returns to Hand; a removed Tactic returns to Reserve.
- The affected player may replace from the same source or pass.
- A revised choice does not create another Surveillance or Interference opportunity.
- Updated Counterintelligence, Conscription, Disinformation, Operational Reassessment, Intercepted Orders, Spies, Reconnaissance, Deep Cover, Assassins, Treason, Subversion, Exfiltration, and related Mission text.

## Mystics

- Reworked the Rite sequence and Ritual victory.
- The first completed Rite unlocks **Invocation**.
- The second completed Rite unlocks **Transmutation**.
- The third completed Rite unlocks **Convergence** and permits beginning the **Ritual of Ascendance**.
- Completing all three Rites no longer wins immediately.
- The Mystic must bind one Arcane card from Hand, Discard Pile, and Graveyard, then initiate and win a battle while all three remain bound.
- Losing a battle before completion interrupts the Ritual; completed Rites remain.
- Withdrawal neither completes nor interrupts the Ritual.
- Revised Black Covenant as a Tactic that binds and plays one Hand-origin additional Tactic.

## Inquisition

- Clarified Conviction timing during the Aftermath.
- Condemnation now sends opposing Tactics to the Graveyard.
- Blasphemy triggers when an opposing Arcane card is played; face-down battle cards trigger when revealed.
- Final Judgment occurs after the battle cards are cleared, allowing the normal Conviction gain first.
- Relentless Pursuit occurs at the end of the Aftermath.
- Revised Tyranny priority, Heresy copied-effect timing, Hellfire, No Martyrs, and the Graveyard-pressure cards.
- Reworked Confession as a Tactic-stage information and self-replacement effect.

## Military

- Clarified normal Command timing before victory-dependent Orders.
- Revised all Orders for the formal battle and Aftermath sequence.
- Reworked Brothers in Arms as a Tactic-oriented Hand/Reserve combination effect.
- Revised Reserve Force, Hold the Line, Give Chase, Countercharge, War Crimes, Shock and Awe, and other cards for additional Tactics and follow-up battles.
- Rearguard now prevents the same Order or specific physical card from being used again that turn after its movement is prevented. A second copy of the same title remains legal.

## Financiers

- Replaced phase language with Action Opportunity timing.
- Clarified that Deed ownership is independent of token Position, Territory control, Assets, and Overlays.
- Clarified Asset replacement interactions with Underwriting, Property Dues, Margin Loan, and Tariffs.
- Revised Hostile Takeover, Speculation, Monetary Crisis, Capital Gains, Margin Loan, Leveraged Buyout, Foreclosure, Property Dues, and Corner the Market for the formal Aftermath sequence.

## Neutral cards and Territories

- Updated all affected Neutral cards for Gambits, Reserves, Tactics, Aftermath, withdrawal, copied effects, and Asset/Overlay language.
- Updated all affected Territories and Arenas for Gambits, Reserves, Tactics, and the Aftermath.
- Watchtower now changes only Gambit order and face state.
- Poisonous Gas limits each player to one total Gambit or Tactic in the battle and moves Tactics to the Graveyard.
- Grand Melee grants an additional initial Reserve card and one additional Tactic.

## Rules Arbiter and playtest infrastructure

- Rules Arbiter answers must identify the rules version and classify the ruling as **Explicit**, **Inferred**, or **Unresolved**.
- Answers must cite the governing source and must not invent precedence.
- Added a review-record requirement for questions, answers, citations, classifications, and corrections.
- Implemented unique single-use QR codes for printed formal-playtest sheets.
- Each printed sheet is assigned a live digital session before printing; scans join that pre-existing session.
- Rules Arbiter questions are linked to the session automatically.
- Closing the session retires the QR code.
- A human-readable sheet serial provides fallback reconciliation.

## Unchanged release structure

Unless specifically revised above, v0.6.1 retains:

- six factions and twelve Leaders;
- 50 Neutral cards and 72 faction cards;
- 25 Territories, including four Arenas;
- Deck construction of at least 30 cards and no more than 60 total value;
- exactly three different Territories with no more than one Arena;
- the distinction between Occupation and control;
- capture at the start of the occupier's next turn; and
- running the Gauntlet as the universal victory route.
