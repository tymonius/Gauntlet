# Gauntlet v0.6.4 Candidate — Archived Release-Candidate Record

**Status:** Archived release candidate; never published as a numbered public release.

**Archived:** August 23, 2026

`v0.6.4-candidate` was the final current-development rules and component source bundle assembled on top of the published v0.6.3 baseline before the project promoted that work to the v0.7.0 product release.

This record exists for provenance and historical reference. It does **not** retroactively make v0.6.4 a published release, and no `v0.6.4-candidate` Git tag or GitHub Release should be created from it.

## Candidate contents

At the archived snapshot, the candidate authority resolved to:

- 142 playable cards;
- 25 Territories;
- six factions;
- twelve Leaders; and
- twelve recommended starter Decks.

The candidate also contained the current Onset battle model, the v0.6.4 card additions and text changes, updated Territory set, Diplomat Proposals, Arcane-symbol clarification, current faction/Leader rules, and the component contract used by the developing Tabletop Simulator package.

## Provenance

The historical snapshot is pinned to repository commit:

`268b6b3bfd6a5d3bf6ac97de815ab7af93cd69b3`

The authority entrypoint at that commit is:

`game-data/current-game.json`

The machine-readable archive manifest in this directory records the source paths and known Git blob identities used by that authority.

## Relationship to v0.7.0

The approved `v0.6.4-candidate` bundle was **promoted into v0.7.0**, rather than published under the v0.6.4 number. v0.7.0 preserves `v0.6.4-candidate` as its source provenance while adding the assembled product/release identity appropriate to the illustrated-card and Tabletop Simulator milestone.

Accordingly, this archive should be read as the historical release-candidate state immediately preceding v0.7.0, not as a separate public playtest edition.
