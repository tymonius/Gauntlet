# Early Tabletop Simulator archive

This archive records recovered original Gauntlet / “Tymon's Game” Tabletop Simulator saves and saved objects from 2020–2021.

The earliest surviving save is dated **September 2, 2020 at 3:32:39 AM**, making September 2 the earliest documented date of the playable project. The recovered files were rediscovered in the original local TTS Saves and Saved Objects directories in September 2026.

## Preservation policy

Original JSON files and their matching TTS PNG thumbnails are historical artifacts. Preserve their original filenames and byte content where possible. Do not reserialize, normalize, update hosted asset URLs, or otherwise modernize them merely to make them conform to current repository conventions.
