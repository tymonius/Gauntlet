Gauntlet v0.6.3

Gauntlet v0.6.3 is a preserved historical playtest edition with six factions, twelve Leaders, 128 playable cards, 25 Territories, twelve competitive starter Decks, and its published Rulebook and reference files. It was superseded by v0.7.0.
