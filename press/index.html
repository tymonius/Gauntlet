Short description

Gauntlet is a two-player tactical card-and-territory game about constructed decks, hidden battle commitments, territorial control, sharply asymmetric factions, and breaking through the opponent's line.

Current facts

  • Current canonical playtest edition: v0.7.1
  • 2 players
  • 6 factions and 12 Leaders
  • 142 playable cards
  • 25 Territories and Arenas
  • Physical print-and-play and Tabletop Simulator support
  • Created and developed by Tymon Scott
  • Published by TDS Games, an imprint of Misty Hollow Enterprises
