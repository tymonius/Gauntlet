# Financiers Capital Ledger

Production component notes for the v0.6.4 Financiers Capital Ledger.

- Poker-card size, matching the reference-card shell.
- Duplex component with the same ledger face printed on both sides.
- Public transaction record with Entry, ±, and Balance columns.
- Opening Balance is preprinted as 2.
- A faint Declaration Pro example row demonstrates usage: `Income | +1 | 3`.
- Eleven additional ruled rows remain blank for handwriting.
- The ledger grid begins exactly at the former `Public Capital Record` divider position; side padding is unchanged, and the recovered vertical space is distributed through the ledger rows.
- Footer metadata is `FINANCIERS | LEDGER | v0.6.4` through the current-game display version.
- The ledger is intended to be printed on ordinary paper and consumed during play; a second identical face provides a second use or overflow space.
