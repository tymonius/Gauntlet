# v0.6.4 Territory / Arena brevity review

**Issue:** #738  
**Baseline:** `artifacts/reconstruction/clean-v0.6.3/complete-authority/territories.json`  
**Classification:** approved v0.6.4 wording review; includes explicitly approved clarifications and terminology repairs noted below  
**Published v0.6.3:** immutable and unchanged

## Review rules

This pass uses the reconstructed v0.6.3 Territory authority as the current-language baseline rather than the older `docs/Gauntlet_v0.6_Territory_Pool.md`, which still contains superseded terms such as Battle Hand, Defender's Advantage, withdraw, and Smuggler's Pass.

The wording below is the card-by-card language approved in #748. Shared v0.6.3 language conventions are applied where their semantics are established, including `+1 Reserve`, `+1 Tactic`, `+1 Card`, `Retreat +1`, `Asset` without redundant `banked`, natural `In the Aftermath` wording, and capitalization of defined terms.

Approved wording in this document is not yet the published game authority. It must be promoted into a v0.6.4 candidate Territory source and propagated downstream before release.

## Standard Territories

### 1. Quicksand

**Current**
> If a player begins their Movement on Quicksand, they cannot voluntarily Fall Back or move more than one Position that turn. Forced displacement is unaffected.

**Approved**
> A player who begins their Movement here cannot Advance or Fall Back more than one Position or use an effect to increase their movement that turn. Retreat is unaffected.

**Semantic review:** intentional clarification. The approved wording explicitly prevents movement-increasing effects from circumventing the one-Position cap and names the defined Retreat procedure directly.

### 2. Poisonous Gas

**Current**
> During a battle on Poisonous Gas, each player may set a Gambit or choose a Tactic, but not both. A player cannot have more than one Gambit or Tactic in that battle.  
> A Tactic chosen during that battle goes to its owner's Graveyard during the Aftermath of the battle instead of their Discard Pile. If a player chooses no Tactic, they put one unchosen card from their initial Reserve in their Graveyard during the Aftermath, if able.

**Approved**
> During battles here, each player may employ Gambits or Tactics, but not both. In the Aftermath, Tactics go to the Graveyard. A player who chose no Tactic puts 1 card from their Reserve in the Graveyard.

**Semantic review:** intentional clarification. The restriction is category exclusivity, not a one-card ceiling; all Tactics used in the battle go to the Graveyard; and the no-Tactic penalty applies to 1 card from the player's Reserve.

### 3. Difficult Terrain

**Current**
> When a player enters Difficult Terrain, their movement ends. A player who begins their turn there or enters it during their turn cannot play a card for its Action effect during Denouement that turn.

**Approved**
> When a player enters Difficult Terrain, their movement ends. A player who begins their turn here or enters it that turn cannot play a card for its Action effect during Denouement.

**Semantic review:** wording-only.

### 4. Garrison

**Current**
> When Garrison's controller defends it, they draw one additional card when forming their initial Reserve.

**Approved**
> While Garrison's controller is defending here: +1 Reserve.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. `+1 Reserve` is the established shorthand for drawing one additional card when forming Reserve.

### 5. Disrupted Supply Lines

**Current**
> While a player occupies Disrupted Supply Lines, only 1 of their Assets can be active. They choose which one remains active; their other Assets are inactive until they leave.

**Approved**
> While a player is here, only 1 of their Assets can be active. They choose which.

**Semantic review:** terminology repair restoring the original symmetric mechanic. Earlier versions explicitly applied while any player was on the Territory; the later use of `occupies` became accidentally asymmetric once Occupation was defined specifically as being on an opposing uncontrolled Territory.

### 6. Ruined Storehouse

**Current**
> Once during their Draw step, a player occupying Ruined Storehouse may draw the top card of their Discard Pile instead of drawing from their Draw Pile.

**Approved**
> Once during their Draw step, a player here may draw the top card of their Discard Pile instead of a card from their Draw Pile.

**Semantic review:** terminology repair. The effect applies to the player physically here regardless of control; current defined Occupation is narrower than that.

### 7. Field Hospital

**Current**
> During the Aftermath of a battle on Field Hospital, when the battle cards are cleared, its controller may put one card they controlled in that battle that would enter their Graveyard in their Discard Pile instead.

**Approved**
> In the Aftermath of a battle here, its controller may put 1 of their cards from that battle in their Discard Pile instead of their Graveyard.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. Avoids using `controlled` for ordinary battle cards.

### 8. Exposed Flank

**Current**
> When Exposed Flank's controller counterattacks an opponent occupying it, that opponent cannot set a Gambit.

**Approved**
> During a Counterattack here, the occupier cannot set a Gambit.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. Uses the defined Counterattack/occupier relationship directly.

### 9. High Ground

**Current**
> The defending player in a battle on High Ground gains advantage.

**Approved**
> During battles here, the defender gains Advantage.

**Semantic review:** wording-only plus defined-term capitalization.

### 10. Fortified Pass

**Current**
> When Fortified Pass's controller defends it, the attacking player's banked Assets are inactive during that battle.

**Approved**
> While Fortified Pass's controller is defending here, the attacker's Assets are inactive.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. `Asset` already means a banked Asset.

### 11. Insurgency

**Current**
> While an opponent occupies Insurgency without controlling it, their banked Assets are inactive.

**Approved**
> While a player is in Occupation here, their Assets are inactive.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. Uses the defined Occupation state and removes redundant `banked`.

### 12. Watchtower

**Current**
> When Watchtower's controller defends it, the attacker sets their Gambit face up or passes. The defender then sets their Gambit normally or passes.

**Approved**
> While Watchtower's controller is defending here, the attacker must set their Gambit first, face up.

**Semantic review:** approved compression. Attacker-first sequencing and face-up information remain explicit; normal Gambit rules supply the option to pass and the defender's subsequent choice.

### 13. Supply Depot

**Current**
> During the Draw step, a player occupying and controlling Supply Depot draws one additional card as part of their normal draw.

**Approved**
> If Supply Depot's controller starts their turn here, +1 Card.

**Semantic review:** intentional timing clarification/change. Qualification now occurs at the start of the controller's turn rather than being phrased as a Draw-step presence condition.

### 14. Old Battlefield

**Current**
> During the Aftermath of a battle on Old Battlefield, when cards remaining in Reserve move to the Discard Pile, its controller may put one unchosen card from their Reserve in their Graveyard instead.

**Approved**
> In the Aftermath of a battle here, its controller may put 1 Reserve card in their Graveyard instead of their Discard Pile.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. A card still in Reserve is necessarily unchosen under the normal battle procedure.

### 15. Refuge

**Current**
> After a player voluntarily Falls Back onto Refuge, they draw one card.

**Approved**
> After a player Falls Back or withdraws to this Territory: +1 Card.

**Semantic review:** intentional mechanic expansion. Withdrawal to Refuge now triggers the draw in addition to Fall Back; Retreat does not.

### 16. Command Tent

**Current**
> If a player begins their turn occupying and controlling Command Tent, they may take one Action during both Opening and Denouement that turn. If they do, both Actions may be used only to play cards for their Action effects.

**Approved**
> If Command Tent's controller starts their turn here, +1 Action. They may take Actions during both Opening and Denouement that turn. Their first Action in each phase must be used to play a card for its Action effect.

**Semantic review:** interaction clarification. The effect grants the additional Action and permits Actions in both phases without incorrectly capping other effects that may permit multiple same-phase Actions; the first Action in each phase carries Command Tent's card-play restriction.

### 17. Monastery

**Current**
> While Monastery's controller occupies it, cards cannot leave either player's Graveyard. During battles on Monastery, cards with the Arcane trait have no effect.

**Approved**
> While Monastery's controller is here, cards cannot leave either player's Graveyard. During battles here, Arcane cards have no effect.

**Semantic review:** terminology repair plus wording compression. `controller is here` expresses physical presence without misusing the defined Occupation term.

### 18. Training Grounds

**Current**
> When Training Grounds's controller defends it, after forming their initial Reserve but before choosing Tactics, they may discard that entire Reserve and draw the same number of replacement cards. Those cards become their Reserve.

**Approved**
> While Training Grounds's controller is defending here, before choosing Tactics, they may discard their Reserve and draw the same number of cards to form a new Reserve.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. Preserves the timing, quantity, optionality, and replacement-Reserve status.

### 19. King's Road

**Current**
> A player who begins their turn on King's Road gains one additional position of movement during that turn's Movement step.

**Approved**
> A player who starts their turn here may move 1 additional Position during Movement that turn.

**Semantic review:** wording-only.

### 20. Toll Bridge

**Current**
> To voluntarily advance from Toll Bridge, a player must discard one card from their Hand. If they cannot, they cannot advance from it.

**Approved**
> Players must discard 1 card from Hand to voluntarily Advance from this Territory.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. The general cost rules already establish that an unpaid required cost prevents the voluntary Advance.

### 21. Smuggler's Run

**Current**
> During Opening or Denouement, as an Action, while occupying and controlling Smuggler's Run, a player may stash one card from their Hand face down beneath it. The stashed card does not count toward the Hand limit.  
> While that player occupies and controls Smuggler's Run, they may play the stashed card for its Action effect or set it as a Gambit as though it were in their Hand, if eligible. It counts as a card played or set from Hand.  
> At the start of the stashing player's turn, if they control Smuggler's Run, they may return the stashed card to their Hand. If they lose control of Smuggler's Run, put the stashed card in its owner's Discard Pile. Only one card may be stashed here.

**Approved**
> During Opening or Denouement, as an Action, this Territory's controller, while here, may stash 1 card from Hand face down beneath it.  
> While here and controlling this Territory, they may play the stashed card for its Action effect or set it as a Gambit.  
> At the start of their turn, if they control this Territory, they may return the stashed card to Hand. If they lose control, it is discarded.

**Semantic review:** terminology repair and approved compression. The impossible `occupying and controlling` wording is replaced with physical-presence-plus-control language. Hand-limit, single-card, ordinary legality, and source-identity explanations are omitted as unnecessary edge-case restatements.

## Arenas

The shared rules already require a Tiebreak Roll whenever tied totals are not resolved by Defensive Edge or another applicable tie-breaking rule. Because every Arena removes Defensive Edge, repeating the Tiebreak Roll instruction on every Arena is redundant and has been removed.

### 22. Arena: Spoils of War

**Current**
> During battles on Spoils of War, Defensive Edge does not apply. If battle totals remain tied, make a Tiebreak Roll.  
> During the Aftermath of the battle, when cards remaining in Reserve move to the Discard Pile, the winner may put one unchosen card from their Reserve in their Hand instead.

**Approved**
> During battles here, Defensive Edge does not apply. In the Aftermath, the winner may put 1 Reserve card in their Hand instead of their Discard Pile.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. The Tiebreak Roll follows automatically from the shared rules once Defensive Edge is removed.

### 23. Arena: No Quarter

**Current**
> During battles on No Quarter, Defensive Edge does not apply. If battle totals remain tied, make a Tiebreak Roll. The losing player retreats one additional position, if able.

**Approved**
> During battles here, Defensive Edge does not apply. Losing player: Retreat +1, if able.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. The Tiebreak Roll follows automatically from the shared rules.

### 24. Arena: Single Combat

**Current**
> During battles on Single Combat, Defensive Edge does not apply. If battle totals remain tied, make a Tiebreak Roll. All banked Assets are inactive during the battle.

**Approved**
> During battles here, Defensive Edge does not apply. All Assets are inactive.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. Removes redundant `banked`, battle-duration restatement, and Tiebreak instruction.

### 25. Arena: Grand Melee

**Current**
> During battles on Grand Melee, Defensive Edge does not apply. If battle totals remain tied, make a Tiebreak Roll. Each player draws one additional card when forming their initial Reserve and may choose one additional Tactic from it.

**Approved**
> During battles here, Defensive Edge does not apply. Each player: +1 Reserve, +1 Tactic.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. `+1 Reserve` and `+1 Tactic` use established battle shorthand; the Tiebreak Roll follows automatically from the shared rules.

## Approved non-editorial / terminology decisions

The review identified several places where merely compressing the v0.6.3 sentence would preserve an obsolete or unintended interaction. These are explicitly approved for v0.6.4 rather than being silently treated as editorial identity:

- **Quicksand:** movement-increasing effects cannot circumvent its one-Position voluntary movement cap.
- **Poisonous Gas:** Gambit/Tactic restriction is category exclusivity; all Tactics go to Graveyard; the no-Tactic penalty uses 1 Reserve card.
- **Disrupted Supply Lines:** restored to its original symmetric effect for any player here after `Occupation` became a narrower defined term.
- **Ruined Storehouse / Monastery / Smuggler's Run:** repaired obsolete generic uses of occupation language.
- **Supply Depot:** qualification is at the start of the controller's turn.
- **Refuge:** Withdrawal to Refuge now also triggers the draw.
- **Command Tent:** clarified interaction with additional/same-phase Actions.
- **Arenas:** redundant Tiebreak Roll instructions removed because the shared rules already supply that procedure when Defensive Edge does not resolve a tie.

## Density / risk notes

- **Smuggler's Run** remains the density outlier, but the approved wording removes edge-case explanations that the shared rules already supply.
- **Poisonous Gas**, **Command Tent**, and **Smuggler's Run** deserve particular semantic test coverage because their approved wording encodes the most interaction-sensitive behavior.
- Production renders should still specifically inspect Smuggler's Run despite its substantial compression.
