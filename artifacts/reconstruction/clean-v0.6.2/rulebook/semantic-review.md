## Shared-rule review

- Setup is v0.6.2-specific: draw four, keep three, place the fourth face down beneath the Draw Pile, then arrange Territories with the opening Hand known.
- Player Tokens begin immediately before their own-end Territory; setup placement is not movement or entry.
- Turn order is `Capture → Draw → Opening → Movement → Denouement → Cleanup`.
- Faction Actions and Faction Abilities remain distinct.
- Additional Actions do not create new Action phases or same-phase permission unless text says so directly.
- Movement uses Advance / Hold / Fall Back; withdrawal is reserved for leaving a pending or active battle without a winner.
- Pre-battle timing is `pending battle → Terms → Onset`; accepted Terms prevent the battle from beginning.
- Defensive Edge resolves eligible tied battle totals; otherwise the Tiebreak Roll is a separate unmodified one-die sudden-death procedure.
- Territory control is contiguous from each player's own end through the Front Line; deep Occupation does not create isolated control.
- Normal v0.6.2 victory remains cumulative: final-Territory control is required before the normal Last Stand, and final-Territory capture alone does not win.
- The recovered specificity rule is present without allowing specificity to retroactively undo an effect already applied.
- Integrated Rulebook cross-references point to Chapters 5, 7, and 8 rather than the standalone shared-candidate section numbers.

## Faction review

All six faction chapters are sourced from the PR #609 authority set and preserve the twelve Leader ownership mappings:

- Military — General: Onward / Rally / Rout; Commandant: Entrench / Repel / Fortify.
- Diplomats — Ambassador: Cordiality; Senator: Political Capital.
- Financiers — Banker: Line of Credit; Executive: Hostile Takeover.
- Intelligence — Ranger: Fieldcraft; Spymaster: Mission Control.
- Mystics — Alchemist: Materia Prima; Spirit Walker: Guardians of the Circle.
- Inquisition — Grand Inquisitor: Final Judgment; Witch Hunter: Relentless Pursuit.

The Rulebook retains faction engines and Leader rules but does not duplicate the canonical faction card catalogs.

Checked v0.6.2 faction deltas include 2 starting Capital for Financiers, triangular Diplomat Leverage, Denouement Intelligence mission Actions, thirteen Arcane Mystics cards, and the Opening/Denouement Inquisition Purge exception.

## Cross-version exclusions

The candidate does not adopt v0.6.3-only changes, including:

- face-up opening discard;
- starting on the own-end Territory;
- independent final-Territory and Last Stand normal victories;
- v0.6.3 Deck / Draw Pile terminology cleanup;
- Asset-only / inherent-Bank presentation rules;
- `Second Line` or `Smuggler's Run` title migrations;
- v0.6.3 shared card-language rules;
- v0.6.3 competitive starter Decks.

## Approval effect

Merging the Rulebook reconstruction PR approves this self-contained Rulebook as part of the clean v0.6.2 authority set for certification. It does **not** publish v0.6.2, make it current, or unlock clean v0.6.3 by itself.
