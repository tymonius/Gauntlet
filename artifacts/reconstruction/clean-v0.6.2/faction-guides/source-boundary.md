# Source boundary

The clean v0.6.2 faction authority candidates are synthesized from:

- the six definitive v0.6.1 faction guides;
- the approved v0.6.2 shared-rule and faction/component candidate decisions;
- the v0.6.2 compatibility audit;
- PR #511 same-phase Action corrections; and
- the version-scoped reconstruction plan approved through PRs #606 and #607.

The withdrawn v0.6.2 Rulebook and combined faction/component guide are forbidden as authority skeletons. They may be consulted only as historical evidence when a primary decision source requires comparison.

No clean-v0.6.3 delta is permitted into this candidate set before clean v0.6.2 authority is certified.
