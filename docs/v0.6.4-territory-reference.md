# Gauntlet v0.6.4 Candidate Territory Reference

> **Candidate reference only.** This document is derived from `docs/v0.6.4-territories.json`, approved under issue #738. Published v0.6.3 remains unchanged until a v0.6.4 release cutover.

## Standard Territories

### 1. Quicksand
> A player who begins their Movement here cannot Advance or Fall Back more than one Position or use an effect to increase their movement that turn. Retreat is unaffected.

### 2. Poisonous Gas
> During battles here, each player may employ Gambits or Tactics, but not both. In the Aftermath, Tactics go to the Graveyard. A player who chose no Tactic puts 1 card from their Reserve in the Graveyard.

### 3. Difficult Terrain
> When a player enters Difficult Terrain, their movement ends. A player who begins their turn here or enters it that turn cannot play a card for its Action effect during Denouement.

### 4. Garrison
> While Garrison's controller is defending here: +1 Reserve.

### 5. Disrupted Supply Lines
> While a player is here, only 1 of their Assets can be active. They choose which.

### 6. Ruined Storehouse
> Once during their Draw step, a player here may draw the top card of their Discard Pile instead of a card from their Draw Pile.

### 7. Field Hospital
> In the Aftermath of a battle here, its controller may put 1 of their cards from that battle in their Discard Pile instead of their Graveyard.

### 8. Exposed Flank
> During a Counterattack here, the occupier cannot set a Gambit.

### 9. High Ground
> During battles here, the defender gains Advantage.

### 10. Fortified Pass
> While Fortified Pass's controller is defending here, the attacker's Assets are inactive.

### 11. Insurgency
> While a player is in Occupation here, their Assets are inactive.

### 12. Watchtower
> While Watchtower's controller is defending here, the attacker must set their Gambit first, face up.

### 13. Supply Depot
> If Supply Depot's controller starts their turn here, +1 Card.

### 14. Old Battlefield
> In the Aftermath of a battle here, its controller may put 1 Reserve card in their Graveyard instead of their Discard Pile.

### 15. Refuge
> After a player Falls Back or withdraws to this Territory: +1 Card.

### 16. Command Tent
> If Command Tent's controller starts their turn here, +1 Action. They may take Actions during both Opening and Denouement that turn. Their first Action in each phase must be used to play a card for its Action effect.

### 17. Monastery
> While Monastery's controller is here, cards cannot leave either player's Graveyard. During battles here, Arcane cards have no effect.

### 18. Training Grounds
> While Training Grounds's controller is defending here, before choosing Tactics, they may discard their Reserve and draw the same number of cards to form a new Reserve.

### 19. King's Road
> A player who starts their turn here may move 1 additional Position during Movement that turn.

### 20. Toll Bridge
> Players must discard 1 card from Hand to voluntarily Advance from this Territory.

### 21. Smuggler's Run
> During Opening or Denouement, as an Action, this Territory's controller, while here, may stash 1 card from Hand face down beneath it.
>
> While here and controlling this Territory, they may play the stashed card for its Action effect or set it as a Gambit.
>
> At the start of their turn, if they control this Territory, they may return the stashed card to Hand. If they lose control, it is discarded.

## Arenas

Shared battle rules already require a Tiebreak Roll when a tied battle is not resolved by Defensive Edge or another tie-breaking rule. The Arena cards therefore need only remove Defensive Edge; they do not repeat the Tiebreak Roll procedure.

### 22. Arena: Spoils of War
> During battles here, Defensive Edge does not apply. In the Aftermath, the winner may put 1 Reserve card in their Hand instead of their Discard Pile.

### 23. Arena: No Quarter
> During battles here, Defensive Edge does not apply. Losing player: Retreat +1, if able.

### 24. Arena: Single Combat
> During battles here, Defensive Edge does not apply. All Assets are inactive.

### 25. Arena: Grand Melee
> During battles here, Defensive Edge does not apply. Each player: +1 Reserve, +1 Tactic.
