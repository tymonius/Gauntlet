# v0.6.4 Territory / Arena brevity review

**Issue:** #738  
**Baseline:** `artifacts/reconstruction/clean-v0.6.3/complete-authority/territories.json`  
**Classification:** editorial candidate only; no mechanics changes approved or applied  
**Published v0.6.3:** immutable and unchanged

## Review rules

This pass uses the reconstructed v0.6.3 Territory authority as the current-language baseline rather than the older `docs/Gauntlet_v0.6_Territory_Pool.md`, which still contains superseded terms such as Battle Hand, Defender's Advantage, withdraw, and Smuggler's Pass.

Every proposed rewrite below is intended to preserve timing, subject, control/occupation requirements, optionality, destinations, battle-role restrictions, Reserve/Tactic source, and `if able` conditions exactly. Shared v0.6.3 language conventions are applied only where their semantics are already established, including `+1 Reserve`, `+1 Tactic`, `+1 Card`, `Retreat +1`, `Asset` without redundant `banked`, natural `In the Aftermath` wording, and capitalization of the defined term `Advantage`.

No candidate wording in this document is authoritative until separately approved and propagated.

## Standard Territories

### 1. Quicksand

**Current**
> If a player begins their Movement on Quicksand, they cannot voluntarily Fall Back or move more than one Position that turn. Forced displacement is unaffected.

**Approved**
> A player who begins their Movement here cannot Advance or Fall Back more than one Position or use an effect to increase their movement that turn. Retreat is unaffected.

**Semantic review:** approved wording intentionally clarifies the mechanic. It caps voluntary Advance/Fall Back movement at one Position, prevents movement-increasing effects from circumventing that cap, and names Retreat directly instead of using the broader phrase `forced displacement`.

### 2. Poisonous Gas

**Current**
> During a battle on Poisonous Gas, each player may set a Gambit or choose a Tactic, but not both. A player cannot have more than one Gambit or Tactic in that battle.  
> A Tactic chosen during that battle goes to its owner's Graveyard during the Aftermath of the battle instead of their Discard Pile. If a player chooses no Tactic, they put one unchosen card from their initial Reserve in their Graveyard during the Aftermath, if able.

**Approved**
> During battles here, each player may employ Gambits or Tactics, but not both. In the Aftermath, Tactics go to the Graveyard. A player who chose no Tactic puts 1 card from their Reserve in the Graveyard.

**Semantic review:** approved wording clarifies the intended category restriction rather than imposing a one-card ceiling: a player may employ Gambits or Tactics, but never both types in the same battle. All Tactics used in the battle go to the Graveyard in the Aftermath. The no-Tactic penalty now applies to any card remaining in that player's Reserve rather than only an unchosen card from the initial Reserve; this is an intentional rules clarification rather than a purely editorial compression.

### 3. Difficult Terrain

**Current**
> When a player enters Difficult Terrain, their movement ends. A player who begins their turn there or enters it during their turn cannot play a card for its Action effect during Denouement that turn.

**Candidate**
> When a player enters Difficult Terrain, their movement ends. A player who begins their turn there or enters it that turn cannot play a card for its Action effect during Denouement.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. The same-turn Denouement restriction remains explicit.

### 4. Garrison

**Current**
> When Garrison's controller defends it, they draw one additional card when forming their initial Reserve.

**Candidate**
> When Garrison's controller defends it: +1 Reserve.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. `+1 Reserve` is the established shorthand for drawing one additional card when forming Reserve.

### 5. Disrupted Supply Lines

**Current**
> While a player occupies Disrupted Supply Lines, only 1 of their Assets can be active. They choose which one remains active; their other Assets are inactive until they leave.

**Candidate**
> While a player occupies Disrupted Supply Lines, only 1 of their Assets can be active. They choose which.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. The opening `while` clause already supplies the duration and makes the final `until they leave` restatement redundant.

### 6. Ruined Storehouse

**Current**
> Once during their Draw step, a player occupying Ruined Storehouse may draw the top card of their Discard Pile instead of drawing from their Draw Pile.

**Candidate**
> Once during their Draw step, a player occupying Ruined Storehouse may draw the top card of their Discard Pile instead of a card from their Draw Pile.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. Preserves the once-per-Draw-step replacement and top-of-Discard-Pile requirement.

### 7. Field Hospital

**Current**
> During the Aftermath of a battle on Field Hospital, when the battle cards are cleared, its controller may put one card they controlled in that battle that would enter their Graveyard in their Discard Pile instead.

**Candidate**
> In the Aftermath of a battle on Field Hospital, its controller may put 1 card they controlled in that battle in their Discard Pile instead of their Graveyard.

**Semantic review:** wording-only candidate, relying on the shared Aftermath card-clearing procedure for the exact cleanup moment. The eligible controller-owned battle card and Graveyard-to-Discard replacement remain unchanged.

### 8. Exposed Flank

**Candidate**

### 9. High Ground

**Candidate**

**Semantic review:** wording-only plus current defined-term capitalization.

### 10. Fortified Pass

**Current**

**Candidate**

**Semantic review:** wording-only. `Asset` already means a banked card under current language conventions.

### 11. Insurgency

**Candidate**

### 12. Watchtower

**Current**

**Candidate**
> When Watchtower's controller defends it, the attacker sets their Gambit face up or passes; then the defender sets theirs normally or passes.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. Preserves attacker-first order, face-up information, and each player's option to pass.

**Candidate**

**Candidate**

**Semantic review:** wording-only. Retains the unchosen-Reserve eligibility and Discard-to-Graveyard replacement while relying on shared Aftermath cleanup timing.

### 15. Refuge

**Candidate**

### 16. Command Tent

**Current**

**Candidate**

**Semantic review:** wording-only. Preserves start-of-turn qualification, one Action in each named window, and the restriction on both Actions.

### 17. Monastery

**Candidate**

### 19. King's Road

**Candidate**

### 20. Toll Bridge

**Current**

**Candidate**

### 21. Smuggler's Run

**Current**
> During Opening or Denouement, as an Action, while occupying and controlling Smuggler's Run, a player may stash one card from their Hand face down beneath it. The stashed card does not count toward the Hand limit.  
> While that player occupies and controls Smuggler's Run, they may play the stashed card for its Action effect or set it as a Gambit as though it were in their Hand, if eligible. It counts as a card played or set from Hand.  
> At the start of the stashing player's turn, if they control Smuggler's Run, they may return the stashed card to their Hand. If they lose control of Smuggler's Run, put the stashed card in its owner's Discard Pile. Only one card may be stashed here.

**Candidate**
> During Opening or Denouement, as an Action, while occupying and controlling Smuggler's Run, a player may stash 1 card from Hand face down beneath it. It does not count toward their Hand limit. Only 1 card may be stashed here.  
> While that player occupies and controls Smuggler's Run, they may play the stashed card for its Action effect or set it as a Gambit as though it were in Hand, if eligible. It counts as played or set from Hand.  
> At the start of the stashing player's turn, if they control Smuggler's Run, they may return the stashed card to Hand. If they lose control, put it in its owner's Discard Pile.

**Semantic review:** wording-only candidate. The explicit Opening/Denouement restriction is intentionally retained rather than inferred from generic Action timing. Occupation/control, face-down stash state, Hand-limit exception, one-card limit, Action/Gambit eligibility, Hand-source identity, optional return, and loss-of-control cleanup all remain explicit.

## Arenas

The common Arena battle boilerplate remains printed on every Arena in this pass. Centralizing it into a new shared Arena rule would be a rules-architecture change, not a wording-only edit.

### 22. Arena: Spoils of War

**Current**
> During battles on Spoils of War, Defensive Edge does not apply. If battle totals remain tied, make a Tiebreak Roll.  
> During the Aftermath of the battle, when cards remaining in Reserve move to the Discard Pile, the winner may put one unchosen card from their Reserve in their Hand instead.

**Candidate**
> During battles on Spoils of War, Defensive Edge does not apply. If battle totals remain tied, make a Tiebreak Roll. In the Aftermath, the winner may put 1 unchosen card from their Reserve in their Hand instead of their Discard Pile.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. The winner's unchosen-Reserve destination replacement remains explicit.

### 23. Arena: No Quarter

**Current**
> During battles on No Quarter, Defensive Edge does not apply. If battle totals remain tied, make a Tiebreak Roll. The losing player retreats one additional position, if able.

**Candidate**
> During battles on No Quarter, Defensive Edge does not apply. If battle totals remain tied, make a Tiebreak Roll. Losing player: Retreat +1, if able.

**Semantic review:** wording-only, using established retreat shorthand while preserving `if able`.

### 24. Arena: Single Combat

**Current**
> During battles on Single Combat, Defensive Edge does not apply. If battle totals remain tied, make a Tiebreak Roll. All banked Assets are inactive during the battle.

**Candidate**
> During battles on Single Combat, Defensive Edge does not apply. If battle totals remain tied, make a Tiebreak Roll. All Assets are inactive during the battle.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. Removes redundant `banked` only.

### 25. Arena: Grand Melee

**Current**
> During battles on Grand Melee, Defensive Edge does not apply. If battle totals remain tied, make a Tiebreak Roll. Each player draws one additional card when forming their initial Reserve and may choose one additional Tactic from it.

**Candidate**
> During battles on Grand Melee, Defensive Edge does not apply. If battle totals remain tied, make a Tiebreak Roll. Each player: +1 Reserve, +1 Tactic.

**Semantic review:** wording-only. `+1 Reserve` preserves the extra initial Reserve draw, and `+1 Tactic` uses Reserve as the established default Tactic source.

## Density / risk notes

- **Smuggler's Run** remains the clear density outlier after safe compression because its mechanic genuinely needs stash timing, source identity, access modes, return timing, control-loss cleanup, and a one-card limit.
- **Poisonous Gas** is now approved as an intentional rules clarification: it restricts players to employing Gambits or Tactics rather than both categories, sends all Tactics to the Graveyard in the Aftermath, and applies the no-Tactic penalty to a card from the player's Reserve.
- **Command Tent**, **Training Grounds**, and **Spoils of War** should receive particular semantic and production-fit scrutiny because their text encodes timing or replacement behavior that should not be compressed into bare shorthand.
- The four Arenas share substantial boilerplate, but removing it from the cards is intentionally out of scope for a wording-only pass.
