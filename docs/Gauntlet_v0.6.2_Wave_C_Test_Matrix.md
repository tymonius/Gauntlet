# B. Leader strategy and signature package

## B01 — Military / General — Forward Doctrine identity

## B03 — Diplomats / Ambassador — Open Channels identity

## B08 — Intelligence / Spymaster — Mission Network identity

# E. Shared first-game and tableside procedures

## E01 — Turn sequence
**Expect:** The active-player reference uses Capture → Draw → Opening → Movement → Denouement → Cleanup and permits the normal Action during Opening or Denouement.

## E02 — Action permissions
**Expect:** An additional Action does not itself permit two Actions in one phase, and Faction Actions retain their listed phases.

## E03 — Pending battle and Terms
**Expect:** The reference uses Pending battle → Terms → Onset → Gambits; accepted Terms prevent Onset and the battle.

## E04 — Active battle sequence
**Expect:** The reference uses Onset → set Gambits → form Reserves → reveal Gambits → choose Tactics → reveal Tactics → Outcome → Aftermath.

## E05 — Physical battle zones
**Expect:** Hand, Gambit, Reserve, and Tactic are visibly separate and arranged in play order.

## E06 — Bound cards
**Expect:** A bound card is shown beneath or beside its binding effect, outside normal zones, unusable except through that effect, with destination determined by the binding.

## E07 — Movement vocabulary
**Expect:** Fall Back is ordinary Movement, retreat follows loss, and withdrawal ends or prevents battle without a winner.

## E08 — Occupation and Front Line
**Expect:** The demonstration distinguishes attacker Occupation from later Capture and shows contiguous Front Line advancement.

## E09 — Active-player marker
**Expect:** The marker says YOUR TURN, carries the six-step turn sequence, and is passed after Cleanup without tracking priority or battle initiative.

## E10 — Guided dry run
**Expect:** The five-minute demonstration uses sample Gambit and Reserve cards, ignores printed effects, demonstrates result movement and destinations, then resets and shuffles.

## E11 — Faction reference authority
**Expect:** Each player receives one self-contained faction reference and only a concise opponent summary for the opposing faction.

## E12 — Release boundary
**Expect:** Wave C leaves the published v0.6.1 Deckbuilder and /start/ catalog unchanged until Wave D propagation.
