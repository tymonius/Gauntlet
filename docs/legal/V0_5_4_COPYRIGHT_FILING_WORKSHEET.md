### Author recollection of distribution

On 2026-08-28, the author recalled:

- the other player was probably allowed to keep the cards;
- printed rulebooks were handed out.

This creates a plausible June 21 publication event because ownership/lending of physical copies can constitute publication. But the Copyright Office's limited-publication doctrine means the event may remain nonpublication if the copies were confined to a selected playtest group for a limited purpose and recipients lacked the right to diffuse/reproduce/distribute them.
