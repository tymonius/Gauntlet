## Human authorship to claim

Because much of the exact 2026 rules/card prose was generated or consolidated through ChatGPT, this filing should **not initially claim all new text as human-authored**.

The strongest presently documented human authorship is compilation/editorial:

**Working `Author Created — Other`:**

> Selection, coordination, and arrangement of rules, card/reference material, deck lists, and playtest materials; qualifying human revisions.

Do not check a broad `Text` claim merely to capture functional rules language. Add `Text` only if the final provenance review identifies a sufficient body of specific human-authored expressive text in the deposit.

### Human-authorship evidence

The retained June 19–20 development conversation documents the human author making or selecting substantive design alternatives, including:

- the battle-card source/destination lifecycle;
- retaining permanent cost for hand commitments while recycling battle-draw cards;
- concentrating harsher loser attrition in the Attrition card rather than the universal battle rule;
- the three-card Battle version;
- the overall physical-playtest package objective;
- repeated simulation/revision decisions governing what entered the canonical v0.5.3 corpus.

These facts support human selection/coordination and design authorship. They are not treated as proof that the human personally wrote every final sentence.

## Material Excluded

Working `Material Excluded / Other`:

> Previously published Gauntlet material; text generated by artificial intelligence; pictorial material; third-party material; uncopyrightable game methods, procedures, systems, titles, and short phrases.

### Scope notes

**Previously published Gauntlet material**
- includes appreciable expression carried from the 2020–2021 game;
- does not automatically include every internal June 2026 draft merely because it predates v0.5.3.

**Text generated by artificial intelligence**
- includes appreciable final prose supplied by ChatGPT and retained without sufficient human expressive modification;
- does not include material merely proofread, formatted, or mechanically processed by AI where the human supplied the expression.

**Pictorial material**
- excludes visual material wholesale from this literary filing;
- visual/card-art authorship will be handled in a separate workstream.

**Game methods/systems**
- excludes the underlying methods of play and procedures regardless of who designed them.
