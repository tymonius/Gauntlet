# Gauntlet v0.5.4 — Targeted Text Authorship Audit

**Prepared:** 2026-08-29  
**Purpose:** Decide whether the v0.5.4 foundation application should include a broad `Text` authorship claim in addition to compilation/editorial authorship.

## Conclusion

**Do not check a broad `Text` authorship claim for v0.5.4.**

The retained June 17–21 development record strongly supports Tymon Scott's human authorship of the game's design choices, selection, coordination, arrangement, and qualifying revisions. It does **not** presently identify a sufficiently appreciable body of independently expressive prose personally written by Tymon and carried substantially verbatim into the v0.5.4 Complete Playtest Guide to justify making `Text` a principal authorship claim.

This is a conservative filing choice, not a conclusion that no sentence in the deposit contains any human-authored expression.

## Evidence reviewed

The retained development record shows a recurring pattern:

1. Tymon identifies a gameplay problem, requirement, or design preference.
2. ChatGPT proposes or drafts exact rule/card prose implementing that direction.
3. Tymon accepts, rejects, corrects, or redirects the substance.
4. ChatGPT incorporates the accepted result into the canonical package.

The v0.5.4 Last Stand change is a clear example:

- ChatGPT proposed the substantive rule and exact wording.
- Tymon's responsive instruction was essentially approval: `Do it. And push 0.5.4.`
- The resulting Guide contains the assistant-drafted Last Stand rule.

Other June 2026 evidence similarly establishes strong human control over **what the game does**, including:

- source-based battle-card destinations;
- keeping hand commitments as permanent cost while recycling battle-draw cards;
- placing harsher loser attrition in the Attrition card rather than the universal battle rule;
- selecting the three-card Battle implementation;
- deciding the package scope and physical-playtest format.

Those contributions are highly relevant to human design and compilation/editorial authorship, but they are not the same as personally supplying the final protectable sentence-level expression.

## Why functional rules prose does not justify a forensic sentence audit

The v0.5.4 Complete Playtest Guide is dominated by:

- procedures;
- game systems;
- timing instructions;
- component definitions;
- card effects;
- short functional reference text.

The underlying methods, procedures, and systems are outside copyright protection. Some particular rule wording can still contain protectable expression, but the copyright value of reconstructing sentence-by-sentence provenance for every concise functional instruction is low.

The stronger and cleaner v0.5.4 claim is therefore the human-authored **selection, coordination, and arrangement** of the complete playtest edition, plus qualifying human revisions actually embodied in the final work.

## Application consequence

### Author Created

Use **Other**:

> selection, coordination, and arrangement of rules, card/reference material, deck lists, and playtest materials; qualifying human revisions

Do **not** check `Text` for the v0.5.4 foundation application.

### Material Excluded

Use:

> Previously published Gauntlet material; text generated by artificial intelligence; pictorial material; third-party material; uncopyrightable game methods, procedures, systems, titles, and short phrases.

### New Material Included

Use the same description as `Author Created`:

> selection, coordination, and arrangement of rules, card/reference material, deck lists, and playtest materials; qualifying human revisions

## Relationship to later filings

This narrow v0.5.4 foundation claim does not prevent later Gauntlet registrations from claiming genuinely human-authored new prose.

For future Rulebook editions, the preferred provenance workflow is:

- use AI for analysis, consistency checking, organization, and editorial critique;
- have Tymon supply the final expressive prose for sections whose literary wording is commercially important;
- preserve that authorship record;
- register the resulting new human-authored text in the appropriate later revised-work application.

## Filing status

This targeted review removes the final substantive authorship question from the v0.5.4 foundation package.

The v0.5.4 Standard Application should proceed as a **Literary Work compilation/editorial claim without a broad Text checkbox claim**.
