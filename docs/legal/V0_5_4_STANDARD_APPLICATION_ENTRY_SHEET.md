## 6. Limitation of Claim

### Material Excluded

Use `Other` for the complete limitation statement:

`Previously published Gauntlet material; text generated by artificial intelligence; pictorial material; third-party material; uncopyrightable game methods, procedures, systems, titles, and short phrases.`

Working meaning:

- **Previously published Gauntlet material**: appreciable expression carried from the public 2020–2021 Gauntlet work.
- **Text generated by artificial intelligence**: appreciable final expression supplied by a generative system and retained without sufficient human expressive modification.
- **Pictorial material**: all images/graphics in this Literary Work filing; visual authorship is reserved for a separate workstream.
- **Third-party material**: any independently owned material, if present.
- **Game methods/procedures/systems**: underlying gameplay methods and functional systems, which are outside the literary claim regardless of who designed them.

Do **not** exclude v0.5.0–v0.5.3 merely because those internal development stages preceded v0.5.4. The retained development record supports treating them as unpublished, unregistered stages owned by the same claimant and incorporated into the first published v0.5.4 foundation edition.
