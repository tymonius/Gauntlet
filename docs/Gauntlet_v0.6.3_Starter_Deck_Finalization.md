# Gauntlet v0.6.3 — Starter Deck Finalization

**Status:** Final starter Deck set for the v0.6.3 release.  
**Machine-readable source:** `v0.6.3/data/starter-decks-candidate.js`  
**Prior design audit:** `docs/Gauntlet_v0.6.3_Strong_Starter_Decks_Second_Pass_Audit.md`

## Release decision

The twelve recommended starter Decks are finalized for v0.6.3. They remain optimized for competitive strength, winning capability, and strong or creative Leader strategies. Teaching simplicity and card-pool coverage are not optimization targets.

The second-pass competitive audit remains the design record for the broad rebuild. After the v0.6.3 corrections to Armistice, Manifest Destiny, and Contingency Plan, the remaining excluded cards and unresolved slot questions were challenged one final time. Only the revised **Contingency Plan** won new starter slots.

No further theory-only starter changes should be made for v0.6.3. Future composition changes require playtest evidence.

## Final post-audit changes

### Military — Commandant — Holdfast

- **Unbroken Ranks ×3 → ×2**
- **Contingency Plan ×0 → ×1**

The third Unbroken Ranks has diminishing returns because it only pays after a win in which no Order was used, while Command is capped at 2 and Commandant frequently wants to spend Orders. Contingency Plan gives the defensive shell a high-efficiency stabilization card when the opponent has gained the Territory lead: at cost 1 its Gambit/Tactic effect is +2 Battle Total while behind on Territory control. One copy gives the Deck a strong comeback tool without overloading it with a conditional effect.

### Inquisition — Witch Hunter — Relentless Pursuit

- **Scouting Report ×1 → ×0**
- **Contingency Plan ×0 → ×1**

Witch Hunter already has three Confessions for commitment information and Tactic revision. The lone Scouting Report is therefore less important than a cost-1 +2 Battle Total effect precisely when the Witch Hunter is behind on Territories. That directly supports the Deck's central plan: turn a threatened defensive collapse into a defensive win, end the opponent's turn through Relentless Pursuit, and counteradvance.

## Cards rechecked after the v0.6.3 corrections

The corrected **Armistice** remains out of the baseline. Moving its upkeep to the start of Opening closes the unintended draw-suppression loophole but does not remove its substantial Hand tax or the tension between its battle lock and strategies that ultimately need to initiate a battle.

**Manifest Destiny** remains out of the baseline. Its added Territory is explicitly a normal Territory with a normal Deed under the existing Financier rules, which makes it function correctly as an intentional structural Financier counter. That does not make its cost-5, build-around effect more generally efficient than the signature cards occupying current starter slots.

The revised **Contingency Plan** is no longer classified as underpowered. Its final text is strong enough to earn one copy in Commandant and one in Witch Hunter, but its Territory-control condition is sufficiently binary that additional copies are not justified without matchup evidence.

## Final construction result

All twelve release Decks are exactly **30 cards / 60 Deckbuilding Value**, with no Unique card above one copy and exactly three different Territories per player with at most one Arena.

The final twelve Decks collectively use **110 of 128 playable titles (85.9%)**. This coverage is incidental; it was not an optimization target.

The **18 titles not used by any v0.6.3 starter Deck** are:

- Armistice
- Bombardment
- Capital Punishment
- Conscription
- Counterworks
- Insurrection
- Liberation
- Manifest Destiny
- Monetary Crisis
- Requisition
- Revolution
- Rousing Speech
- Salvage
- Sedition
- Sequestration
- Strategic Withdrawal
- Valor
- War Crimes

Their exclusion is not a balance judgment by itself. The final audit found them to be specialist, matchup-dependent, high-variance, build-around, or simply weaker than an incumbent for these twelve particular baseline strategies.

## Playtest gate for future changes

Further starter revisions should be driven by actual games rather than another broad theory pass. Useful evidence includes:

- opening-hand dead-card rate;
- cards repeatedly stranded in Hand at game end;
- realized value per copy;
- cards consistently discarded or banked only as generic value rather than used for their text;
- alternate-victory frequency and timing;
- matchup-specific cards that are either essential or routinely dead;
- repeated failure of a Deck to execute its intended Leader strategy;
- cards that consistently dominate the slot or never justify their Deckbuilding Value.

The finalized v0.6.3 starter lists should therefore be treated as the fixed release baseline until playtest data gives a concrete reason to change them.
