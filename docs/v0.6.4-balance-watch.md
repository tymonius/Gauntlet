# Gauntlet v0.6.4 Balance Watch

## Diplomats — Peace Treaty

For v0.6.4 playtesting, Peace Treaty requires **six different ratified Proposals** instead of five.

No other part of the Diplomat engine changes in this adjustment. Terms, Influence, Proposal requirements and effects, ratification routes, offer timing, and Peace Treaty victory timing remain unchanged. This change does not add a final Peace Treaty task or capstone.

### Playtest watch

Diplomats are undefeated in the current playtest sample. Treat the six-Article threshold as a narrow balance adjustment and evaluate it before making deeper changes to Terms or adding a separate Peace Treaty finale.

Record the existing Diplomat watch metrics in `Gauntlet_Playtest_Targets_and_Metrics.md`, with particular attention to:

- the turn when Peace Treaty first becomes a credible threat;
- Treaty Articles ratified by route;
- Terms offered, accepted, refused, and imposed;
- whether the sixth Article meaningfully increases opponent interaction and response time; and
- Diplomat win rate and victory route after the threshold change.
