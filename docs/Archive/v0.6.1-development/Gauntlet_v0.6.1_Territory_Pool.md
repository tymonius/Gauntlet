# Gauntlet v0.6.1 Territory Pool

> **Definitive v0.6.1 Territory source.** This document governs the canonical names, complexity, and exact player-facing text of all 25 Territories: 21 standard Territories and four Arenas.

## General Territory rules

- All Territories are revealed during setup and normally remain face up.
- A Territory's printed effect is active unless its text, an exposed Overlay, or another effect states otherwise.
- Control and occupation matter only when a Territory specifically refers to them.
- If an effect suppresses a Territory's printed effect, all of that printed effect is inactive for the stated duration.
- Overlays and Ruins follow the shared Overlay rules in the core rulebook.
- When a Territory changes control, control of each Overlay on it changes with the Territory. The Overlay's owner does not change.

# Standard Territories

## 1. Quicksand

**Complexity:** Basic

> If a player begins their Movement step on Quicksand, they cannot voluntarily withdraw or move more than one position that turn. Forced displacement is unaffected.

## 2. Poisonous Gas

**Complexity:** Advanced

> During a battle on Poisonous Gas, each player may set a Gambit or choose a Tactic, but not both. A player cannot have more than one Gambit or Tactic in that battle.
>
> A Tactic chosen during that battle goes to its owner's Graveyard during the Aftermath of the battle instead of their Discard Pile. If a player chooses no Tactic, they put one unchosen card from their initial Reserve in their Graveyard during the Aftermath, if able.

## 3. Difficult Terrain

**Complexity:** Basic

> When a player enters Difficult Terrain, their movement ends. A player who begins their turn there or enters it during their turn cannot play a card for its Action effect during the Action Opportunity after movement that turn.

## 4. Garrison

**Complexity:** Basic

> When Garrison's controller defends it, they draw one additional card when forming their initial Reserve.

## 5. Disrupted Supply Lines

**Complexity:** Advanced

> While a player occupies Disrupted Supply Lines, only one banked Asset they control can be active. They choose which one remains active; all their other banked Assets are inactive until they leave.

## 6. Ruined Storehouse

**Complexity:** Basic

> Once during their Draw step, a player occupying Ruined Storehouse may draw the top card of their Discard Pile instead of drawing from their Draw Pile.

## 7. Field Hospital

**Complexity:** Basic

> During the Aftermath of a battle on Field Hospital, when the battle cards are cleared, its controller may put one card they controlled in that battle that would enter their Graveyard in their Discard Pile instead.

## 8. Exposed Flank

**Complexity:** Basic

> When Exposed Flank's controller counterattacks an opponent occupying it, that opponent cannot set a Gambit.

## 9. High Ground

**Complexity:** Basic

> The defending player in a battle on High Ground gains advantage.

## 10. Fortified Pass

**Complexity:** Basic

> When Fortified Pass's controller defends it, the attacking player's banked Assets are inactive during that battle.

## 11. Insurgency

**Complexity:** Basic

> While an opponent occupies Insurgency without controlling it, their banked Assets are inactive.

## 12. Watchtower

**Complexity:** Basic

> When Watchtower's controller defends it, the attacker sets their Gambit face up or passes. The defender then sets their Gambit normally or passes.

## 13. Supply Depot

**Complexity:** Basic

> During the Draw step, a player occupying and controlling Supply Depot draws one additional card as part of their normal draw.

## 14. Old Battlefield

**Complexity:** Basic

> During the Aftermath of a battle on Old Battlefield, when cards remaining in Reserve move to the Discard Pile, its controller may put one unchosen card from their Reserve in their Graveyard instead.

## 15. Refuge

**Complexity:** Basic

> After a player voluntarily withdraws onto Refuge, they draw one card.

## 16. Command Tent

**Complexity:** Advanced

> If a player begins their turn occupying and controlling Command Tent, they may gain 1 additional Action that turn. If they do, both Actions may be spent only to play cards for their Action effects, one during each normal Action Opportunity.

## 17. Monastery

**Complexity:** Advanced

> While Monastery's controller occupies it, cards cannot leave either player's Graveyard. During battles on Monastery, cards with the Arcane trait have no effect.

## 18. Training Grounds

**Complexity:** Advanced

> When Training Grounds's controller defends it, after forming their initial Reserve but before choosing Tactics, they may discard that entire Reserve and draw the same number of replacement cards. Those cards become their Reserve.

## 19. King's Road

**Complexity:** Basic

> A player who begins their turn on King's Road gains one additional position of movement during that turn's Movement step.

## 20. Toll Bridge

**Complexity:** Basic

> To voluntarily advance from Toll Bridge, a player must discard one card from their Hand. If they cannot, they cannot advance from it.

## 21. Smuggler's Pass

**Complexity:** Advanced

> During an Action Opportunity, while occupying and controlling Smuggler's Pass, a player may spend 1 Action to stash one card from their Hand face down beneath it. The stashed card does not count toward the Hand limit.
>
> While that player occupies and controls Smuggler's Pass, they may play the stashed card for its Action effect or set it as a Gambit as though it were in their Hand, if eligible. It counts as a card played or set from Hand.
>
> At the start of the stashing player's turn, if they control Smuggler's Pass, they may return the stashed card to their Hand. If they lose control of Smuggler's Pass, put the stashed card in its owner's Discard Pile. Only one card may be stashed here.

# Arenas

Arenas are Territories. A Deck may include no more than one Arena among its three selected Territories.

## 22. Arena: Spoils of War

**Complexity:** Basic

> During battles on Spoils of War, Defender's Advantage does not apply. If battle totals are tied, reroll the battle dice.
>
> During the Aftermath of the battle, when cards remaining in Reserve move to the Discard Pile, the winner may put one unchosen card from their Reserve in their Hand instead.

## 23. Arena: No Quarter

**Complexity:** Basic

> During battles on No Quarter, Defender's Advantage does not apply. If battle totals are tied, reroll the battle dice. The losing player retreats one additional position, if able.

## 24. Arena: Single Combat

**Complexity:** Basic

> During battles on Single Combat, Defender's Advantage does not apply. If battle totals are tied, reroll the battle dice. All banked Assets are inactive during the battle.

## 25. Arena: Grand Melee

**Complexity:** Advanced

> During battles on Grand Melee, Defender's Advantage does not apply. If battle totals are tied, reroll the battle dice. Each player draws one additional card when forming their initial Reserve and may choose one additional Tactic from it.
