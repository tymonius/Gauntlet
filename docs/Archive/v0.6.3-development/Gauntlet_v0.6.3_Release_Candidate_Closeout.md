## Promotion boundary

The candidate directory is intentionally outside `releases/`. A later publication pass may promote these files into `releases/v0.6.3/` only after the printed-material package and final cross-surface comparison pass are green.

Until then:

- v0.6.2 remains the published playtest edition;
- public website/current-release links remain on v0.6.2;
- the public Rules Arbiter default remains v0.6.2;
- the digital default remains v0.6.2; and
- `releases/v0.6.3/` must not exist.
