# Malleus Maleficarum trigger revision

The v0.6.4 candidate effect was revised from title-level persistent Arcane classification to a once-per-turn per-card declaration:

> **Asset:** Once per turn, when an opponent plays or reveals a non-Arcane card, you may declare it heretical. It has the Arcane trait for that play or reveal and until the end of the turn.

This preserves the core design that the Inquisition defines witchcraft while making the effect immediate, low-tracking, and directly compatible with Blasphemy timing. The card remains Cost 3 provisionally, with 3 versus 4 still a playtest question.
