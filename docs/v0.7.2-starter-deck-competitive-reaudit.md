# Gauntlet v0.7.2 — Starter Deck Competitive Re-audit

**Status:** Approved v0.7.2 candidate starter baseline after the New Recruits, Financier, Intelligence, and Inquisition changes.

## Objective

Re-run the established competitive starter audit against the current v0.7.2 gameplay authority.

The optimization standard remains the one used for the v0.6.3 competitive rebuild:

- competitive strength and credible winning capability first;
- strong expression of the selected Leader and faction plan second;
- challenge every incumbent slot against the full legal Neutral + faction pool;
- prefer cards that are live often, create tempo/card/battle equity, or materially advance the intended victory route;
- do not optimize for teaching simplicity or card-pool coverage;
- preserve 30 cards / 60 Deckbuilding Value and the current Territory construction contract.

This audit is intentionally conservative where the current list still wins its slot competition. A full re-audit does not imply that all twelve lists must change.

## Current mechanics considered

This pass is against the post-playtest v0.7.2 candidate, including:

- New Recruits at value 2 with “Discard one other card from your Hand if able, then +3 Cards.”
- Financier Treasury during Opening or Denouement, Play the Market during Opening, end-of-Opening Financial Capacity, the revised Deed cost curve, and same-turn Deed surcharge.
- Intelligence recurring Intel equal to Operation Progress and Operational Capacity.
- Inquisition +2 Conviction when two or more opposing cards enter the Graveyard during the first qualifying Aftermath each turn, plus the stronger 1-Conviction Purge.
- the already-completed v0.7.2 competitive Territory-package audit.

## Result

Nine current card lists survive unchanged. Three are revised.

### Military — General — Forward Doctrine

**Result: retain current list.**

The second Pathfinders remains a defensible use of the value released by the New Recruits repricing: the Deck is movement-sensitive, every recommended Territory has meaningful printed text, and Pathfinders remains live as a battle card when that text is active. Reserve Force and War Witch remain plausible A/B candidates, but neither clearly displaces the current package without matchup evidence.

### Military — Commandant — Holdfast

**Result: retain current list.**

The second Contingency Plan is unusually live for a defensive Leader whose strongest turns often begin while behind on Territory control. Restoring the second Encampment would improve Command generation but would require weakening another defensive slot; current evidence does not justify that exchange.

### Diplomats — Ambassador — Open Channels

**Result: retain current list.**

The current shell has sufficient Terms density, acceptance incentives, card flow, and refusal punishment. Contraband remains a credible recursion option, but the present mix of Counterintelligence, Diplomatic Divination, and flexible Neutral interaction is not clearly weaker under the six-Proposal Peace Treaty.

### Diplomats — Senator — Procedure Endures

**Result: retain current list.**

The third Neutral Observers remains acceptable in the refusal-heavy Senator plan. Gunboat Diplomacy remains an A/B candidate, but the current list better protects the Senator’s ability to establish and preserve the Sanctions engine while remaining exactly 60 value.

### Financiers — Banker — Sound Investment

**Result: revise.**

Playtest evidence showed the Banker repeatedly low on Hand cards while cards were being consumed as Treasury/collateral and Capital was preserved for Deeds. The stronger New Recruits helps, but the current emergency legality adjustment also left two Margin Loans in the Deck; a banked Margin Loan suppresses the normal turn draw and can deepen the same liquidity problem.

**Change:**
- Margin Loan ×2 → ×1
- Conscription ×0 → ×1

This is value-neutral. Conscription restores card access plus banking/action compression, while reducing the density of a card that can suppress the Banker’s normal draw. Margin Loan remains a signature tool rather than disappearing from the package.

### Financiers — Executive — Hostile Expansion

**Result: revise.**

The new Financial Capacity structure increases the value of useful Opening Actions because a qualified Financier can still take a qualifying Denouement Action afterward. The emergency New Recruits adjustment had reduced Advance Guard and increased generic Rallying Cry density while leaving the slower Compound Interest engine intact.

**Changes:**
- Compound Interest ×1 → ×0
- Capital Gains ×2 → ×3
- Rallying Cry ×3 → ×2
- Advance Guard ×1 → ×2

The Deck remains 30/60. This shifts one slow passive finance slot toward attack-linked Treasury generation and restores movement pressure that directly supports Hostile Takeover.

### Intelligence — Ranger — Field Operations

**Result: retain current list.**

The third Pathfinders remains core to the hostile-Territory package and saves Intel that can instead be spent on Fieldcraft, Surveillance, or Interference. Recurring Intel improves the faction without making Contraband more important than the current terrain/exploitation density.

### Intelligence — Spymaster — Mission Network

**Result: retain current list.**

Recurring Intel, Operational Capacity, and Mission Control collectively reduce the need for Operational Reassessment and generic additional-Action support. The current higher Exfiltration/Mission density and cheap card access keep the Mission chain moving without restoring the pre-New-Recruits package.

### Mystics — Alchemist — First Principles

**Result: retain current list.**

The added Supplies supports the Hand throughput demanded by repeated Hand-to-Graveyard conversion. Spirit Hollow remains a strong alternate, but the current list already has dense Graveyard access through Dark Omens, Fate’s Toll, Black Covenant, Rend the Veil, Necromancy, Threefold Vision, and the selected Rites.

### Mystics — Spirit Walker — Unbroken Circle

**Result: retain current list.**

The second Scouting Report gives a low-value information/revision card while preserving Deckbuilding Value for the high-value Arcane cards that power Rite progression and Guardians of the Circle. Entrenchment remains an A/B defensive option but is not clearly stronger than the current Arcane-heavy configuration.

### Inquisition — Grand Inquisitor — Final Judgment

**Result: revise.**

The emergency New Recruits legality adjustment removed both Act of Faith and Penance immediately before the post-playtest Inquisition changes made multi-card Aftermath depletion and efficient low-Conviction Purges more valuable. Heresy instead asks the Deck to spend the full 4 Conviction on copying an opposing Graveyard effect, directly competing with the faction’s improved Purification engine.

**Changes:**
- Heresy ×1 → ×0
- Act of Faith ×0 → ×1
- Counterintelligence ×2 → ×1
- Penance ×0 → ×2
- Supplies ×3 → ×2

The Deck remains 30/60. Act of Faith and Penance restore direct depletion/Conviction pressure; Heresy leaves the baseline as the least Purification-focused of the cost-5 Inquisition cards.

### Inquisition — Witch Hunter — Relentless Pursuit

**Result: retain current list.**

The second Contingency Plan remains strongly aligned with the exact game state in which Witch Hunter needs to stabilize: behind on Territories and trying to turn an opposing attack into a defensive win and Relentless Pursuit. Penance, Confession, Court Martial, and Retribution already capitalize on the revised Conviction economy.

## Territory packages

No Territory package changes are made in this pass. The v0.7.2 Territory packages were already re-audited against the rebalanced Quicksand, Supply Depot, and Smuggler’s Run and remain the current competitive recommendations.

## Construction result

All twelve recommended Decks remain:

- exactly 30 playable cards;
- exactly 60 Deckbuilding Value;
- legal for their selected faction;
- within the Unique-card limit; and
- paired with three different Basic Territories and at most one Arena.

## Follow-up playtest watch items

The re-audit does not close the balance questions exposed in live play.

For Banker, continue tracking:
- Hand size at the start and end of each turn;
- cards used for printed effects vs Treasury/collateral;
- Deeds purchased while controlling, occupying, or neither;
- Capital spent on Deeds vs Subsidize;
- whether battle participation remains strategically optional to the point that card text becomes secondary.

For Grand Inquisitor, track:
- first credible Purification threat;
- Conviction gained from one-card vs multi-card Aftermaths;
- Purge frequency and selected cost tier;
- opposing cards permanently depleted per turn.

Further starter changes should use those observations rather than another immediate theory-only pass.
