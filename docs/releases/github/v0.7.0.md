# Gauntlet v0.7.0 — Illustrated Cards & Tabletop Simulator

**Release candidate — not yet published**

Gauntlet v0.7.0 promotes the approved `v0.6.4-candidate` development bundle into the next public playtest edition. It is the first Gauntlet release built around the fully illustrated production card set and the complete Tabletop Simulator package, while also carrying the rules, card-pool, Territory, faction-component, starter-Deck, and player-aid changes developed since v0.6.3.

This is the canonical player-facing changelog from **v0.6.3 → v0.7.0**. Repository-only refactors, CI work, authoring-tool maintenance, and other changes that do not affect play or the player-facing product are intentionally omitted.

## At a glance

- **142 playable cards:** 15 cards enter the current pool and **No Martyrs** is retired, for a net increase from 128 to 142.
- **Battle now begins with Onset:** the separate Pending Battle state is removed; Terms and other pre-Gambit effects now resolve inside the first battle phase.
- **All 25 Territories and Arenas receive the approved v0.6.4 wording pass,** including six deliberate mechanics clarifications or repairs.
- **Peace Treaty now requires six different ratified Proposals,** up from five.
- **All nine Diplomat Proposals receive their approved compressed wording** without changing their underlying Proposal mechanics.
- **Arcane is explicitly a trait rather than faction allegiance:** the Mystics sigil identifies the Arcane trait, while its color reflects the card's allegiance.
- **All 142 playable cards are fully illustrated** in their current production layouts, with current Territory artwork.
- **The physical component system is substantially expanded and finalized:** Universal and faction reference cards, trackers, Proposals/Treaty Articles, Capital Ledgers, Deeds, Rites, and other faction components now have production-ready presentation and print paths.
- **All twelve starter Decks are locked for the release** at 30 cards / 60 Deckbuilding Value, with several updated to incorporate the new card pool.
- **Deckbuilder printing is much more complete:** current cards and components use canonical production renderers, and Advanced → Custom printing can build arbitrary mixed physical-card sheets.
- **Tabletop Simulator arrives as a release feature:** all twelve starter kits and their required physical components are assembled into the v0.7.0 TTS package.

## Returning from v0.6.3?

The core turn structure, six factions, twelve Leaders, Front Line, Run-the-Gauntlet victory routes, and Gambit / Reserves / Tactics battle core remain recognizable. The most important things to relearn are:

1. **There is no separate Pending Battle state.** A battle begins with **Onset**.
2. **Terms resolve first during Onset** when applicable, before other pre-Gambit Onset effects.
3. **The playable card pool is larger:** 15 cards enter the pool and **No Martyrs** is retired.
4. **Peace Treaty requires six different ratified Proposals** rather than five.
5. **Arcane is a trait, not faction allegiance.** The Mystics sigil's shape identifies Arcane; its color identifies allegiance.
6. **Several Territory effects have deliberate mechanical clarifications,** especially Quicksand, Poisonous Gas, Disrupted Supply Lines, Supply Depot, Refuge, and Command Tent.
7. **Several starter Decks changed** to incorporate the new card pool while remaining 30 cards / 60 Deckbuilding Value.

## Battle begins with Onset

v0.7.0 collapses the old Pending Battle state into **Onset**, which is now the first phase of the battle sequence.

The shared sequence is:

1. **Onset**
2. **Gambits**
3. **Reserves**
4. **Tactics**
5. **Outcome**
6. **Aftermath**

During Onset:

1. establish the attacker, defender, contested Position, and attacker's previous Position;
2. resolve Terms first when applicable;
3. if the battle is prevented or a player withdraws, end the battle sequence;
4. resolve other effects that apply during Onset or before Gambits are set; and
5. if the battle still proceeds, continue to Gambits.

If accepted Terms or another effect ends the sequence during Onset, **no battle was fought, won, or lost, and there is no Aftermath**. Refused Terms apply their Refused effect and any refusal modifiers, then the same Onset continues unless that effect ends the sequence. Withdrawal during Onset likewise ends the sequence without a winner or battle result. Once Onset completes and the sequence proceeds to Gambits, later withdrawal uses the normal started-battle, no-result Aftermath and cleanup path.

In a Diplomat mirror, the attacker has the first opportunity to offer Terms. If the attacker passes, the defender may offer. Once either player offers Terms, the other cannot offer Terms in that battle sequence even if the Proposal is refused.

Movement wording is standardized around the same model: entering the opponent's Position initiates a battle and ends that movement sequence. Effect-granted movement may initiate a battle unless the effect says otherwise.

### Existing-card wording updated for Onset

Two released cards receive explicit current-rule wording updates:

- **Advance Guard:** its additional movement may initiate a battle, but if it does, you cannot set a Gambit in that battle.
- **Forced March:** its additional movement cannot initiate a battle.

Leader wording that previously referred to pending battles is likewise updated to the Onset / battle-initiation model without changing the underlying Leader concepts.

## Playable-card pool: 128 → 142

v0.7.0 brings **15 cards** into the current playable pool and retires **one** v0.6.3 card.

### Neutral

- **Phantom Passage** — Arcane movement card that moves you to any Territory you control.
- **Battlefield Plunder** — Gambit/Tactic that can become a Territory Overlay after a win; a player can later clear it during Opening for +2 Cards.

### Military

- **High Command** — banked Asset that raises maximum Command to 3; only one may be banked.
- **War Witch** — Arcane Asset that can be sacrificed while choosing Tactics to gain an additional Tactic from your Graveyard for that battle.

### Diplomats

- **Plenipotentiary** — Unique Asset/Gambit that can convert refused unratified Proposals into ratification at an Influence cost and can reuse another ratified Proposal's Refused effect as a Gambit.
- **Diplomatic Divination** — Arcane Terms effect that predicts whether the opponent will accept or refuse and rewards a correct prediction with Influence.

### Financiers

- **War Bonds** — banked Asset that can place a Hand card face up in Treasury after the first battle each turn for +1 Capital.
- **Actuarial Alchemy** — Arcane Asset that can convert the margin of a battle or Tiebreak Roll loss into Capital.

### Intelligence

- **Regime Change** — banked Asset that prevents the opponent from using Leader abilities; only one may be banked.
- **Spectral Surveillance** — Arcane Asset that can replace Surveillance's Intel cost and reveal all opposing Gambits or all opposing Tactics chosen for that battle.

### Mystics

- **Reembodiment** — Arcane Asset that can recur a lower-value card from the Graveyard after the first qualifying Arcane sacrifice from Hand each turn.
- **Threefold Vision** — Arcane Action that looks at the top three cards of the Draw Pile and sends one each to Hand, Discard Pile, and Graveyard.

### Inquisition

- **Retribution** — Asset that punishes an opponent after they lose a battle they initiated, forcing an Asset loss or increasing Conviction.
- **Anathema** — Asset with a special banking Action that moves an opposing Discard-Pile card to their Graveyard and can later prevent opposing Graveyard recovery.
- **Malleus Maleficarum** — Asset that can declare a non-Arcane opposing card heretical, giving it the Arcane trait for that play or reveal and through the end of the turn.

### Retired

- **No Martyrs** is retired from the active pool. Its v0.6.3 record remains preserved in the historical release/archive, and **Malleus Maleficarum** occupies its replacement slot in the current Inquisition pool.

The resulting v0.7.0 pool contains **142 playable cards: 52 Neutral cards and 15 cards in each faction**. The finalized v0.7.0 card faces and canonical data govern exact costs and wording at release.

## Territory and Arena pass

The complete current set remains **25 cards: 21 standard Territories and 4 Arenas**. All 25 receive the approved v0.6.4 wording pass, while stable identities and numbering are preserved.

Six changes are specifically recorded as mechanical clarifications or repairs:

- **Quicksand:** a player who begins Movement there cannot Advance or Fall Back more than one Position **or use an effect to increase their movement** that turn. Retreat is unaffected.
- **Poisonous Gas:** during battles there, each player may employ **Gambits or Tactics, but not both**. Tactics go to the Graveyard in the Aftermath, and a player who chose no Tactic puts one Reserve card in their Graveyard.
- **Disrupted Supply Lines:** its restriction is symmetric again: while a player is there, only one of **their** Assets can be active, and that player chooses which.
- **Supply Depot:** the +1 Card bonus now keys specifically to **Supply Depot's controller starting their turn there**.
- **Refuge:** it now rewards **Withdrawal** to the Territory as well as Fall Back: after either, +1 Card.
- **Command Tent:** if its controller starts the turn there, they receive +1 Action, may take Actions during both Opening and Denouement that turn, and the first Action in each phase must be used to play a card for its Action effect.

The remaining Territory/Arena revisions standardize and compress wording while preserving the accepted effect. Arena cards no longer need to restate the shared Tiebreak Roll procedure after removing Defensive Edge.

## Diplomats: Proposals, Treaty Articles, and Peace Treaty

### Peace Treaty threshold

The Diplomat **Peace Treaty** victory condition now requires **six different ratified Proposals**, increased from five. Terms procedure, Influence, Proposal requirements/effects, ratification routes, and victory timing otherwise retain their established structure except for the Onset timing migration described above.

### Proposal wording pass

The complete nine-Proposal set receives an approved wording/compression pass. This source is explicitly **wording-only** and does not change the underlying Proposal mechanics:

1. **De-escalation**
2. **Orderly Withdrawal**
3. **Capitulation**
4. **Open Channels**
5. **Mutual Disarmament**
6. **Prisoner Exchange**
7. **Rebuilding Pact**
8. **Ultimatum**
9. **Diplomatic Recognition**

Each Proposal is represented physically with its ratified **Treaty Article** reverse. Terms now resolve during Onset: accepted Terms end the battle sequence during Onset, while refused Terms apply their Refused effect and continue the same Onset unless the effect ends the sequence.

## Arcane identification

The Arcane symbol receives a single explicit visual/rules grammar:

- **Arcane is a trait, not faction allegiance.**
- Mystics cards are Arcane, and cards from other pools may also have the trait.
- Arcane cards are marked with the **Mystics sigil**.
- The sigil's **shape** identifies the Arcane trait.
- The sigil's **color** reflects the card's allegiance, with Neutral cards using the Neutral color.

This is a visual-identification and rules-clarification change rather than a new underlying mechanic.

## Faction Features and Leader-card language

The release standardizes **Faction Feature** as the umbrella term for shared faction-specific actions, abilities, procedures, passive effects, and other named faction mechanics without implying that every such feature consumes an Action.

**Leader Ability** remains the Leader-specific heading. Leader cards use a consistent information hierarchy for Faction Victory, Leader Ability, and Resource or Progression where applicable, with Action use, timing, costs, and frequency stated alongside the relevant feature.

Notable classification cleanups include:

- Military **Orders** remain the named Leader Ability family.
- Intelligence **Intel** remains a Resource, while **Operation Progress** is Progression.
- Mystics use **Progression — Rites** rather than presenting Rites as a Resource.
- Shared faction procedures live primarily on the appropriate Reference cards instead of being redundantly repeated across Leader cards.

These taxonomy and presentation changes are primarily about making the physical components easier to read; they do not by themselves create new gameplay rules unless the current rule or card text expressly changes an effect.

## Starter Decks

v0.7.0 locks one recommended starter Deck for each of the twelve Leaders. Every list remains **30 cards / 60 Deckbuilding Value**. The optimization policy remains competitive strength first, Leader expression second, and title diversity as a tiebreaker; Mystics retain an exception where duplicate cards are functional Rite of Echoes infrastructure.

Changes from the v0.6.3 starter set are:

- **General — Forward Doctrine:** Invasion ×2 → ×1; add **War Witch**.
- **Commandant — Holdfast:** unchanged.
- **Ambassador — Open Channels:** Nonbinding Resolution ×2 → ×1; add **Diplomatic Divination**; strategy copy now reflects the six-Proposal Peace Treaty threshold.
- **Senator — Procedure Endures:** Sanctions: Embargo ×2 → ×1; add **Plenipotentiary**.
- **Banker — Sound Investment:** replace Compound Interest with **War Bonds**; Capital Gains ×2 → ×1; add Conscription.
- **Executive:** unchanged.
- **Ranger:** Reconnaissance ×2 → ×1; add **Spectral Surveillance**.
- **Spymaster:** unchanged.
- **Alchemist:** replace Circle of Bones with **Reembodiment**.
- **Spirit Walker:** Grave Ward ×3 → ×2; add **Threefold Vision**, while preserving the two-copy Rite of Echoes infrastructure.
- **Grand Inquisitor — Final Judgment:** replace Guilt by Association with **Anathema**.
- **Witch Hunter — Relentless Pursuit:** replace Tyranny with **Retribution**; the retired No Martyrs ×2 are replaced by **Malleus Maleficarum ×1** and one additional **Court Martial** for Court Martial ×2 total.

## Fully illustrated production set

v0.7.0 is the first release built around the **fully illustrated current playable-card set**. All 142 playable cards use their current production layouts and approved artwork, and the Territory set uses its current artwork and landscape production treatment.

The official Gauntlet wordmark is also part of the current player-facing visual system, with source, PNG, and SVG assets established for consistent use across release surfaces.

## Card Reference and Deckbuilder card inspection

The browser tools now show the **actual production-rendered card faces** rather than maintaining text-only approximations:

- **Card Reference** uses the production playable-card and Territory renderers, including artwork, parchment, typography, footers, and fitting.
- **Deckbuilder** uses the same production-rendered playable-card faces in its detail/preview flow.
- Shared enlarged-card and artwork inspection makes it possible to move from the catalog to the complete card and then to the underlying uncropped artwork.

## Reference cards and faction components

The current physical component package includes a shared, two-sided **Universal Reference Card** in every Deck plus the finalized faction-specific references needed by each faction:

- Diplomat Reference;
- Financier Reference;
- Intelligence Mission Reference;
- Intelligence Operations Reference;
- Mystics Reference;
- Inquisition Doctrine Reference; and
- Inquisition Purge Reference.

Military intentionally has no separate faction reference card. Its necessary persistent information is carried by its Leader and Command components.

The release also includes the finalized sliding tracker system:

- **Command**
- **Influence**
- **Capital Limit**
- **Intel**
- **Operation Progress**
- **Conviction**

The Financier Capital Limit tracker has physical capacity through 15, but **Capital Limit remains rules-uncapped**; 15 is a component limit, not a game-rule maximum.

Other production supplemental components include the nine Diplomat Proposal / Treaty Article pairs, Financier **Capital Ledgers** and landscape **Deeds**, Mystics **Rites** and Ritual material, and Inquisition doctrine / Purge support.

## Canonical card backs

The physical-card back policy is explicit and shared across production printing and TTS:

- playable cards use the universal black Gauntlet back;
- Territories use the universal black Gauntlet back;
- Leaders use their faction-colored Gauntlet back;
- one-sided faction supplemental cards use their faction-colored back; and
- intrinsically two-sided components use their actual reverse face.

Advanced custom printing can still force a different standard back as a deliberate override, but **Use canonical card backs** is the normal policy.

## Deckbuilder and printing

Deckbuilder printing now consumes the same current production renderers used by the rest of the project for playable cards, Territories, Leaders, and finalized supplemental components. This removes the old divide between a current on-screen card and a stale print-only reconstruction.

Starter print packages use the current Leader, Territories, cards, reference material, and faction components, with improved first-page starter guidance and Territory-order presentation.

Financiers also have a dedicated **Capital Ledger** bulk-print path for consumable ledgers.

### Advanced → Custom printing

A new Custom printing mode builds physical print sheets independently of legal Deck construction. It can:

- browse the current physical-card catalog rather than only the current Deck;
- filter by search, card type, and faction;
- add arbitrary quantities of playable cards and physical components;
- **Add all** currently visible filtered cards without disturbing existing quantities;
- mix portrait and landscape cards on the same 9-up Letter sheet;
- include playable cards, Territories, Leaders, References, trackers, Capital Ledgers, Deeds, Proposals / Treaties, Rites, the Mystics Ritual, and other production-ready card-like components;
- preserve intrinsic reverse faces for genuinely two-sided components; and
- apply canonical card backs by default.

The print path remains fail-closed if a requested production face is not ready rather than silently substituting a stale placeholder.

## Browser Rulebook

During the release-candidate period, the Browser Rulebook can switch between the released v0.6.3 rules and the v0.7.0 candidate rules. The candidate view loads the maintained complete v0.7.0 Rulebook authority directly from `rulebook/player-facing/current-rulebook.md`; the released view remains pinned to the published v0.6.3 Rulebook.

The candidate view is shareable through the rules query parameter, and the v0.6.3 Rules Arbiter is hidden while candidate rules are displayed so it cannot silently answer from the wrong ruleset. At final v0.7.0 cutover, the current v0.7.0 rules become the normal public default.

## Tabletop Simulator

v0.7.0 introduces the complete **Tabletop Simulator** package as a release feature.

The release-candidate build is machine-ready, with:

- **28 ready supplemental component definitions**;
- **80 assembled supplemental objects**;
- **75 network assets** staged for the release package; and
- **zero machine-readiness blockers** in the strict package audit.

The generated table includes Red and Blue player hand zones, all six Gauntlet snap positions, battle dice and Player Tokens, and all twelve starter Bags. Each starter package contains its playable Deck, Leader, three Territories, the shared Universal Reference, and the faction components required by that starter.

The TTS implementation intentionally remains a **digital tabletop, not a rules engine**. Players still perform setup, movement, battle resolution, card handling, and faction procedures themselves.

Final Workshop publication remains gated on the versioned **19-check manual TTS QA record**, explicit `approvedForWorkshop: true` signoff, and the required in-game / remote two-player validation. The release machinery cannot promote the Review Scaffold to the final Workshop save or publish the v0.7.0 GitHub Release while that gate is incomplete.

## Printable Rulebook and release package

The v0.7.0 release package carries the current designed Rulebook as a **100-page half-letter booklet**, imposed as **50 Letter-landscape sides on 25 physical sheets** for duplex short-edge printing and saddle stitching.

The versioned package also includes the v0.7.0 canonical-data export, locked starter-Deck export, and source-provenance export alongside the Rulebook source and booklet PDF.

## What remains familiar

v0.7.0 is a substantial product milestone, but it is not a ground-up replacement for v0.6.3. The six factions, twelve Leaders, turn structure, Front Line, two normal Run-the-Gauntlet victory routes, faction identities, starter-Deck model, and Gambit / Reserves / Tactics battle core remain the foundation of play unless the changes above or a current card/component expressly say otherwise.

## Release boundary

This file describes the **v0.7.0 candidate**. It **does not change the repository's current published release**, create or move a Git tag, create a GitHub Release, or publish the Workshop mod by itself.

Until final cutover, **v0.6.3 remains the current published playtest release**. The `v0.6.4-candidate` bundle is preserved as the never-published source-provenance snapshot that feeds this v0.7.0 product release; there will not be a separate numbered v0.6.4 public release.
