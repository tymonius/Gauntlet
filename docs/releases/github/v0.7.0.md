# Gauntlet v0.7.0 — Illustrated Cards & Tabletop Simulator

**Release candidate — not yet published**

Gauntlet v0.7.0 promotes the approved `v0.6.4-candidate` development bundle into the next public playtest edition. It is the first Gauntlet release built around the fully illustrated production card set and the complete Tabletop Simulator package, while also carrying the rules, card-pool, Territory, faction-component, and player-aid changes developed since v0.6.3.

This is the canonical player-facing changelog from **v0.6.3 → v0.7.0**. Repository-only refactors, test infrastructure, authoring-tool maintenance, and other changes that do not affect play or the player-facing product are intentionally omitted.

## At a glance

- **142 playable cards:** 15 new cards enter the pool and `No Martyrs` is retired, for a net increase from 128 to 142.
- **Battle now begins with Onset:** the separate Pending Battle state is removed; Terms and other pre-Gambit effects now resolve inside the first battle phase.
- **The complete 25-Territory set received a current-rules pass:** several difficult movement, Capture, and timing interactions are now explicit.
- **Diplomat Proposals are fully realized physical components:** nine Proposal / Treaty Article pairs support the Peace Treaty game plan, which now requires six different ratified Proposals.
- **Arcane is standardized as a trait:** the Arcane symbol is not a Resource.
- **All 142 playable cards are fully illustrated** in their current production layouts, with current Territory artwork.
- **The physical component system is substantially expanded:** finalized reference cards, trackers, Proposals, Treaty Articles, Capital Ledgers, Deeds, Rites, and other faction components now share production-ready presentation and print paths.
- **Deckbuilder printing is much more complete:** current cards and components use canonical production renderers, and Advanced → Custom printing can build arbitrary mixed physical-card sheets.
- **Tabletop Simulator arrives as a release feature:** all twelve starter kits and their required physical components are assembled into the v0.7.0 TTS package.

## Returning from v0.6.3?

The core turn structure, six factions, twelve Leaders, Front Line, Run-the-Gauntlet victory routes, and Gambit / Reserves / Tactics battle core remain recognizable. The most important things to relearn are:

1. **There is no separate Pending Battle state.** A battle begins with **Onset**.
2. **Terms resolve first during Onset** when applicable, before other pre-Gambit Onset effects.
3. **The playable card pool is larger:** 15 cards have been added and `No Martyrs` has been retired.
4. **Diplomats now have the finalized nine-card Proposal / Treaty Article set,** and Peace Treaty requires six different ratified Proposals.
5. **Arcane is a trait, not a Resource.** Cards can key off the Arcane symbol as a trait.
6. **Several Territory interactions are now explicitly defined,** especially Hidden Archway, Smugglers Tunnel, Judgement Seat, Quick Sand, Gaes of Wrath, and Elysian Fields.

## Battle begins with Onset

v0.7.0 collapses the old Pending Battle state into **Onset**, which is now the first phase of the battle sequence.

The shared sequence is:

1. **Onset**
2. **Gambits**
3. **Reserves**
4. **Tactics**
5. **Outcome**
6. **Aftermath**

During Onset, establish the attacker, defender, contested Position, and attacker origin. If Terms are applicable, resolve them first. Then determine whether the battle proceeds and, if it does, resolve other eligible Onset / pre-Gambit effects before continuing to Gambits.

If accepted Terms or another effect ends the sequence during Onset, **no battle was fought, won, or lost, and there is no Aftermath**. Refused Terms continue the same Onset and may trigger Sanctions. A withdrawal during Onset likewise ends the sequence before a battle is fought. Once Onset completes and the sequence proceeds to Gambits, later withdrawal follows the normal started-battle, no-result cleanup path.

Movement wording and affected card / Leader text have been updated around this model. Moving into an opponent's Position initiates the battle and ends that movement unless an effect expressly permits otherwise.

## Territory and Arena pass

The complete current set remains **25 Territories: 20 standard Territories and 5 Arenas**. v0.7.0 applies the current-rules pass across that set and makes several previously difficult interactions explicit.

Notable clarifications include:

- **Elysian Fields:** progress Resources are spent and returned according to their faction rules.
- **Quick Sand:** its saving-roll timing is integrated into the initial retreat movement; Terrain modifies movement only when an effect explicitly says it does.
- **Gaes of Wrath:** its end-of-movement Action lock is temporary; other game effects continue normally.
- **Judgement Seat:** its direct Capture attempt is a specific exception to normal Capture procedure, including continuity restrictions; Resolution Waterway remains protected.
- **Hidden Archway:** “may not be Passed” prevents movement into, through, or beyond the Position while the restriction is active.
- **Smugglers Tunnel:** traversal uses its special two-entry permission; end-of-Movement effects do not resolve in the middle of the passage, and normal Movement completion occurs only at the final destination.

The Territory set remains divided among the six faction identities, with the current artwork and landscape production presentation used throughout the release.

## Diplomats: Proposals, Treaty Articles, and Peace Treaty

The Diplomat proposal system is now represented by **nine physical Proposal cards**, each with its ratified **Treaty Article** on the reverse:

1. **Mutual Non-aggression Pact**
2. **Commercial Pact**
3. **Open Borders Agreement**
4. **Resource Sharing Accord**
5. **Arms Limitation Agreement**
6. **Shared Assets Agreement**
7. **Strategic Arms Treaty**
8. **Weapons Restriction Accord**
9. **Conflict Resolution Treaty**

Proposals are Pressed as Terms during Onset. If accepted, the Proposal is ratified and the sequence ends during Onset; its Treaty Article remains in play as applicable. If refused, the Proposal is discarded, the battle continues, and the refusal can create the normal Sanctions opportunity.

The Diplomat **Peace Treaty** victory condition now requires **six different ratified Proposals**.

## Arcane is a trait

The Arcane symbol now has one consistent rules meaning: a card showing the Arcane symbol on its Front or Reverse has the **Arcane trait**.

**Arcane is not a Resource.** Cards and effects may check for the Arcane trait — including effects such as **Leyline Confluence** — but players do not gain, spend, or track Arcane as a resource value.

## Faction Features and Leader-card language

The release standardizes **Faction Feature** as the umbrella term for shared faction-specific actions, abilities, procedures, passive effects, and other named faction mechanics without implying that every such feature consumes an Action.

**Leader Ability** remains the Leader-specific heading. Leader cards use a consistent information hierarchy for faction victory, Leader Ability, and Resource or Progression where applicable, with Action use, timing, costs, and frequency stated alongside the relevant feature.

Notable classification cleanups include:

- Military **Orders** remain the named Leader Ability family.
- Intelligence **Intel** is a Resource, while **Operation Progress** is Progression.
- Mystics use **Progression — Rites** rather than presenting Rites as a Resource.
- Shared faction procedures live primarily on the appropriate reference cards instead of being redundantly repeated across Leader cards.

These taxonomy and presentation changes are primarily about making the physical components easier to read; they do not by themselves create new gameplay rules unless the current rule or card text expressly changes an effect.

## Fully illustrated production cards

v0.7.0 is the first release built around the **fully illustrated current playable-card set**. All 142 playable cards use their current production layouts and approved artwork, and the Territory set uses its current artwork and landscape production treatment.

The release also finalizes the broader physical-component visual system rather than treating faction aids as temporary handoffs.

## Reference cards and faction components

The current physical component package now includes a shared, two-sided **Universal Reference Card** in every Deck plus the finalized faction-specific references needed by each faction. The reference system includes:

- Diplomat Reference;
- Financier Reference;
- Intelligence Mission Reference;
- Intelligence Operations Reference;
- Mystics Reference;
- Inquisition Doctrine Reference; and
- Inquisition Purge Reference.

Military intentionally has no separate faction reference card. Its necessary persistent information is carried by its Leader and Command components.

The release also includes the finalized sliding tracker system:

- **Command**
- **Influence**
- **Capital Limit**
- **Intel**
- **Operation Progress**
- **Conviction**

The Financier Capital Limit tracker has physical capacity through 15, but **Capital Limit remains rules-uncapped**; 15 is a component limit, not a game-rule maximum.

Other production supplemental components include the Diplomat Proposal / Treaty Article pairs, Financier **Capital Ledgers** and landscape **Deeds**, Mystics **Rites** and Ritual material, and the Inquisition's doctrine / Purge support.

## Canonical card backs

The physical-card back policy is now explicit and shared across production printing and TTS:

- playable cards use the universal black Gauntlet back;
- Territories use the universal black Gauntlet back;
- Leaders use their faction-colored Gauntlet back;
- one-sided faction supplemental cards use their faction-colored back; and
- intrinsically two-sided components use their actual reverse face.

Advanced custom printing can still force a different standard back as a deliberate override, but **Use canonical card backs** is the normal policy.

## Deckbuilder and printing

Deckbuilder printing now consumes the same current production renderers used by the rest of the project for playable cards, Territories, Leaders, and finalized supplemental components. This eliminates the old divide between a current on-screen card and a stale print-only reconstruction.

Starter print packages use the current Leader, Territories, cards, reference material, and faction components, with improved first-page starter guidance and Territory-order presentation.

Financiers also have a dedicated **Capital Ledger** bulk-print path for consumable ledgers.

### Advanced → Custom printing

A new Custom printing mode builds physical print sheets independently of legal Deck construction. It can:

- browse the current physical-card catalog rather than only the current Deck;
- filter by search, card type, and faction;
- add arbitrary quantities of playable cards and physical components;
- **Add all** currently visible filtered cards without disturbing existing quantities;
- mix portrait and landscape cards on the same 9-up Letter sheet;
- include playable cards, Territories, Leaders, references, trackers, Capital Ledgers, Deeds, Proposals / Treaties, Rites, the Mystics Ritual, and other production-ready card-like components;
- preserve intrinsic reverse faces for genuinely two-sided components; and
- apply canonical card backs by default.

The print path remains fail-closed if a requested production face is not ready rather than silently substituting a stale placeholder.

## Tabletop Simulator

v0.7.0 introduces the complete **Tabletop Simulator** package as a release feature.

The release-candidate build is machine-ready, with:

- **28 ready supplemental component definitions**;
- **80 assembled supplemental objects**;
- **75 network assets** staged for the release package; and
- **zero machine-readiness blockers** in the strict package audit.

The generated table includes Red and Blue player hand zones, all six Gauntlet snap positions, battle dice and Player Tokens, and all twelve starter Bags. Each starter package contains its playable Deck, Leader, three Territories, the shared Universal Reference, and the faction components required by that starter.

The TTS implementation intentionally remains a **digital tabletop, not a rules engine**. Players still perform setup, movement, battle resolution, card handling, and faction procedures themselves.

Final Workshop publication remains gated on the versioned **19-check manual TTS QA record**, explicit `approvedForWorkshop: true` signoff, and the required in-game / remote two-player validation. The release machinery cannot promote the Review Scaffold to the final Workshop save or publish the v0.7.0 GitHub Release while that gate is incomplete.

## Printable Rulebook

The v0.7.0 release package carries the current designed Rulebook as a **96-page half-letter booklet**, imposed as **48 Letter-landscape sides on 24 physical sheets** for duplex short-edge printing and saddle stitching.

## What remains familiar

v0.7.0 is a substantial product milestone, but it is not a ground-up replacement for v0.6.3. The six factions, twelve Leaders, turn structure, Front Line, two normal Run-the-Gauntlet victory routes, faction identities, starter-Deck model, and Gambit / Reserves / Tactics battle core remain the foundation of play unless the changes above or a current card/component expressly say otherwise.

## Release boundary

This file describes the **v0.7.0 candidate**. It **does not change the repository's current published release**, create or move a Git tag, create a GitHub Release, or publish the Workshop mod by itself.
