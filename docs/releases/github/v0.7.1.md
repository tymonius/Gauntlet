# Gauntlet v0.7.1 — Mystics Rites & Deck Import

Published August 30, 2026.

v0.7.1 is a focused playtest update built on the v0.7.0 core. Its main gameplay change is a complete Mystics Rite-package redesign: Mystics now choose three Rites from a six-Rite pool, may keep those choices hidden until each Rite is begun, and build their game package, starter guidance, printing, and Deckbuilder/TTS transfer around that selected trio.

This is the canonical player-facing changelog from **v0.7.0 → v0.7.1**.

## At a glance

- **Mystics now choose exactly three different Rites from a six-Rite pool** during game-package construction.
- **Selected Rites do not have to be revealed at setup.** A Mystic is under no obligation to reveal a selected Rite until beginning it; when a Rite is begun, reveal it.
- **Rite of Echoes** and **Rite of Crossing** are revised.
- **Rite of Blood** is mechanically unchanged; its explanatory reminder is separated for italic presentation.
- **Rite of Shattering, Rite of Consecration, and Rite of Equivalence** join the pool.
- **An active Rite immediately interrupts and resets if a required card, component, position, or other required game state changes so that the Rite can no longer be completed.**
- Completing all three selected Rites still unlocks the **Ritual of Ascension** route.
- The two Mystics starter Decks are retuned around specific three-Rite packages and recommended Rite orders while remaining **30 cards / 60 Deckbuilding Value**.
- The Deckbuilder now treats Rite selection as part of a Mystics game package: it validates exactly three selections, saves them, prints only the selected three, and includes them in Mystics TTS Deck Codes.
- **Deckbuilder → Tabletop Simulator Deck Codes** are available for stable v0.7.1 and later.
- The v0.7.1 authority locks the audited faction/card summaries and printed-heading taxonomy.
- The Rulebook and release package now carry **TDS Games** publication branding, identified as an imprint of **Misty Hollow Enterprises**.

## Mystics: choose three Rites from six

A Mystics game package now includes exactly **three different selected Rites** chosen from:

1. **Rite of Echoes**
2. **Rite of Blood**
3. **Rite of Crossing**
4. **Rite of Shattering**
5. **Rite of Consecration**
6. **Rite of Equivalence**

The selected trio is part of game-package construction, but **disclosure is optional until a Rite is begun**. When you begin a Rite, reveal it. Only the three selected Rites may be begun during that game.

Completing all three selected Rites satisfies the three-Rite requirement for beginning the **Ritual of Ascension**.

### General Rite interruption rule

If an active Rite can no longer be completed because a required card, component, position, or game state is lost or changes, the Rite is **immediately interrupted and resets**.

This applies in addition to each Rite's own printed interruption condition.

## The six Rites

### Rite of Echoes — revised

Begin by binding one card from your Graveyard face up beneath the Rite. Then bind one card from Hand face up beneath it that shares its title with at least one card in your Draw Pile. Search for that matching card, reveal it, shuffle, and place the revealed card on top of the Draw Pile.

On a later turn, complete Echoes after winning a battle in which the Gambit or Tactic effect of a card with the bound Hand card's title was applied. The Graveyard-bound card moves to the Discard Pile and the Hand-bound card goes to the Graveyard.

If you lose a battle before completion, both bound cards go to the Graveyard and the Rite resets.

### Rite of Blood — mechanically unchanged

Begin by putting one card from Hand in your Graveyard.

On a later turn, complete Blood when you win a battle without setting a Gambit or choosing a Tactic.

The reminder that Transmutation, Assets, Overlays, Territory effects, Leader Abilities, and cards from other sources do not by themselves prevent completion is now presented separately as reminder text.

If you lose a battle before completion, the Rite resets.

### Rite of Crossing — revised

The previous Arcane-card cost is removed.

Crossing may be begun during Denouement only after winning a battle that turn that made you the occupier of a Territory the opponent controlled immediately before that battle.

At the start of your next turn, after Capture, complete the Rite if you still occupy or now control that Territory. If you cease to occupy it first, Crossing resets.

### Rite of Shattering — new

Begin by putting one banked Asset in your Graveyard.

In the first battle that reaches dice on a later turn, gain a number of instances of advantage equal to that Asset's value. Win that battle to complete the Rite.

If you lose that battle, Retreat +1 and reset Shattering.

### Rite of Consecration — new

Begin by placing one Overlay from Hand on a Territory without using an Action. That Territory is consecrated while the Rite is active.

On a later turn, win a battle on the consecrated Territory to complete the Rite.

If you lose a battle there before completion, or if the Overlay is removed first, Consecration resets.

### Rite of Equivalence — new

Begin by choosing one card in Hand and one card in your Graveyard with the same value and exchanging them. Keep the card moved to Hand revealed while the Rite is active.

Complete Equivalence when you play that exchanged card for one of its printed effects.

If the exchanged card leaves Hand in any other way, or if you lose a battle before completion, the Rite resets.

Both v0.7.1 Mystics starters remain **30 cards / 60 Deckbuilding Value**, but each now has an authoritative selected Rite package and recommended Rite order.

### Alchemist — First Principles

Selected Rites:

- **Rite of Blood**
- **Rite of Equivalence**

**Equivalence → Echoes → Blood**

The Deckbuilder starter preview and printed starter strategy now show the recommended Rite order alongside the recommended Territory order.

## Deckbuilder Rite-package support

For Mystics, Rite selection is now part of the Deck itself rather than starter-only metadata.

The Deckbuilder:

- shows all six current Rites through the production Rite-card renderer;
- requires exactly three different selected Rites for a valid Mystics package;
- shows the selected count and selected Rite cards in the current Deck package;
- loads the prescribed Rite trio when an official Mystics starter is selected;
- includes Rite selection when determining whether a Deck still matches an official starter;
- persists selected Rites in saved Deck data and JSON import/export;
- prints only the selected three Rites in a Mystics package rather than the full six-Rite inventory; and
- names the selected Rites in print summaries and printing requests.

## Deckbuilder → Tabletop Simulator Deck Codes

Stable v0.7.1 activates compact **TTS Deck Codes** in the Deckbuilder.

A Deck Code carries:

- game version;
- Deck name;
- faction id;
- Leader id;
- playable-card ids and quantities;
- selected Territory ids; and
- for Mystics, the **three selected Rite ids**.

The matching v0.7.1 TTS importer can rebuild a custom starter kit from that code, including the selected Mystics Rites plus **Ritual of Ascension**. The importer uses embedded starter-kit templates rather than depending on the visible official starter Bags remaining untouched on the table.

The Deckbuilder also includes an explicit TTS transfer walkthrough.

### Workshop boundary

The stable web/rules release and the Steam Workshop update remain separate publication gates.

As of the v0.7.1 web release, the public Workshop item remains **v0.7.0** while the stable v0.7.1 TTS artifact completes its own QA and approval process. The v0.7.1 QA record uses delta inheritance for unchanged v0.7.0 surfaces, while fresh v0.7.1 verification remains required for changed surfaces including the stable hosted save, v0.7.1 Rulebook/setup behavior, Mystics Rites and completed faces, importer/handling drills, and final friction resolution.

The Workshop item will be updated in place after that gate passes; the permanent Workshop id remains **3790840635**.

## Authority and presentation audit

v0.7.1 freezes the audited current authority with:

- **142 playable cards total**;
- **52 Neutral cards**;
- **15 cards in each of the six faction pools**;
- **15 Mystics cards**, all Arcane;
- **Ritual of Ascension** as the canonical Ritual name;
- **Plenipotentiary** and **Martyrdom** correctly marked Unique;
- the Diplomat **Peace Treaty** requiring **six different ratified Proposals / Treaty Articles**; and
- the current printed effect-heading set: **Action, Asset, Gambit, Tactic, Gambit/Tactic, Mission, Overlay, Terms, Sanctions, Reaction**.

The heading cleanup does not intentionally change gameplay; it removes hidden presentation transforms so current card data directly stores the headings players actually see.
