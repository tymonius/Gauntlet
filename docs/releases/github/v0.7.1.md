# Gauntlet v0.7.1 — Mystics Rites & Deck Import

Published August 30, 2026.

v0.7.1 is a focused playtest update built on the v0.7.0 gameplay baseline.

## Player-facing changes

- **Mystics now choose three Rites from a six-Rite pool** during game-package construction instead of always using one fixed trio.
- **Rite of Echoes** and **Rite of Crossing** use their approved revised completion requirements.
- **Rite of Blood** keeps its existing rules, with the completed-state presentation separated from the rules text.
- **Rite of Shattering, Rite of Consecration, and Rite of Equivalence** join the available Rite pool.
- A Mystics player still completes exactly three chosen Rites to unlock the Ritual victory route.
- The v0.7.1 Rulebook and release authority incorporate the approved card/faction drift audit: six factions with fifteen faction cards each, fifteen Arcane Mystics cards, the canonical **Ritual of Ascension** name, **Plenipotentiary** and **Martyrdom** as Unique, and the six-Article Peace Treaty threshold.
- The Rulebook now carries the TDS Games / Misty Hollow Enterprises publication branding.

## Tooling changes

- **Deckbuilder → Tabletop Simulator Deck Codes** activate for v0.7.1 and later. A Deck Code carries the game version, Deck name, faction and Leader ids, card ids/quantities, and selected Territories.
- The TTS importer remains governed by the separate TTS release target and manual Workshop QA gate. Publishing v0.7.1 does **not** replace the currently published v0.7.0 Workshop mod until that gate is completed.

## Compatibility

The core v0.7.0 turn structure, battle sequence, 142-card playable pool, Territories, faction identities, Leaders, and starter Decks remain the baseline unless specifically changed above.
