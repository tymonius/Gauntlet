About the project

Gauntlet.

A two-player tactical card-and-territory game built around deck construction, hidden battle commitments, territorial pressure, and sharply asymmetric factions.

Start playing Read the rules
6 factionsTwelve Leaders with distinct systems and play styles.

25 TerritoriesThe battlefield is built from six player-selected Territories.

The game

Each player chooses a faction and Leader, constructs a Deck, selects three Territories, and joins them with the opponent's three Territories to form the Gauntlet. Players advance through that shared battlefield, commit cards secretly in battles, capture enemy-controlled ground, and attempt to break through the opposing line.

Military focuses on command and direct pressure. Diplomats, Financiers, Intelligence, Mystics, and the Inquisition each add their own resources, components, and an additional route to victory while still contesting the same battlefield.

Development

Gauntlet is in active pre-release development and expanded playtesting. The current canonical playtest edition is v0.7.1. Public rules, cards, starter Decks, the Deckbuilder, Rules Arbiter, and Tabletop Simulator package are maintained against the current published authority.

The current development emphasis is broad human playtesting, matchup and faction balance, winner and loser experience, self-serve onboarding, Tabletop Simulator maintenance, and continued work on the rules-aware digital engine.
