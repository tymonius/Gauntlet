# Gauntlet v0.6.1 is now available

Today I released **Gauntlet v0.6.1: First Playtest Revision**.

This is the first update built directly from physical v0.6.0 playtesting. The session confirmed that the six-faction game was moving in the right direction, but it also showed that battles needed clearer terminology, stricter timing, and a rulebook that was easier to learn from at the table.

## What changed

- Battles now distinguish between **Gambits**, **Reserves**, and **Tactics**. The basic memory aid is: **Gambits go to the Graveyard.**
- The rulebook now introduces each major procedure with a plain-language **How it works** explanation, followed by the complete authoritative rules.
- All six factions, twelve Leaders, 122 playable cards, and 25 Territories have been synchronized with the revised battle structure.
- The Browser Rulebook, Card Reference, Deckbuilder, faction sheets, playtest sheet, player mat, and Rules Arbiter have all been updated for v0.6.1.

This release focuses on clarity, timing, onboarding, and consistency rather than broad balance changes. The next priority is completing more physical games and gathering better evidence about game length, faction balance, comeback potential, alternate victories, and rules friction.
