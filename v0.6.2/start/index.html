First-game path

Learn the game in layers.

First learn the shared turn and one ordinary battle. Then learn your own faction. Finally read only the public information needed to play against the opponent.

Teaching order

  1. The shared game
  2. Your faction and Leader
  3. The opponent summary

Do not read both complete faction guides before playing.

Layer 1

The shared turn

Take one Action during either Opening or Denouement. An additional Action does not by itself permit two Actions in one phase.

Capture → Draw → Opening → Movement → Denouement → Cleanup

Capture

Add the next opposing Territory immediately beyond your Front Line if your token is on or beyond it. Normal Capture advances the Front Line by at most one Territory.

Opening

Take your Action now, or save it.

Movement

Advance, Hold, or Fall Back. Entering the opponent's Position creates a pending battle and ends that Movement sequence.

Denouement

Take your Action now if you did not take it during Opening.

Layer 3

What the opponent needs to know

These parallel summaries expose public decision-relevant information without teaching the opponent's entire faction procedure.

Guided demonstration

Run one battle before the game begins

Physically keep Hand, Gambit, Reserve, and Tactic separate.

Pending battle → Terms → Onset → set Gambits → form Reserves → reveal Gambits → choose Tactics → reveal Tactics → Outcome → Aftermath

  1. Put both tokens on the same Position and identify attacker and defender.
  2. Demonstrate that accepted Terms stop before Onset; otherwise begin the battle.
  3. Set one optional Gambit from Hand.
  4. Draw a three-card private Reserve and choose one optional Tactic.
  5. Reveal, resolve, roll, and compare totals.
  6. Use Defensive Edge only when the defender has it. If the tie remains, make a separate unmodified Tiebreak Roll.
  7. Retreat the loser, establish Occupation, resolve the remaining Aftermath, and clear cards.

Movement reminder: Fall Back is ordinary Movement. A losing player retreats. A player who leaves without losing withdraws.

Physical clarity

Zones and bound cards

Battle zones

Hand → Gambit | Reserve → Tactic

Reserve is temporary and private but is never part of Hand. Unchosen Reserve cards do not become Tactics.

Bound cards

BOUND — outside Hand, Draw Pile, Discard Pile, Graveyard, Reserve, Tactic, and Asset Bank.

A bound card can be moved or affected only by the effect that binds it.

Active-player marker

YOUR TURN
Capture → Draw → Opening → Movement → Denouement → Cleanup

Pass it after Cleanup. It tracks neither battle initiative nor Actions remaining.
