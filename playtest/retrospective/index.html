How it works

Recover an untracked playtest.

  1. Enter your faction, Leader, and the date the game was played.
  2. Share the resulting join link with the other player.
  3. Record the shared result from memory.
  4. Each player submits their own private questionnaire.

Do not recreate live activity. Add only what the players actually remember, and use the journal’s reconstructed-note labels when recording specific moments afterward.
