    Step 2 · First-game introduction

    Learn the game in play order.

    This follows Part I of the Rulebook in the same order. It teaches the ordinary first-game flow; the complete rules remain available for exact timing and exceptions.

    The shape of the game

    Advance. Fight. Hold the ground you take.

    Gauntlet is a two-player tactical card-and-territory game. Each player uses a prepared Deck, chooses one of six factions and one of that faction's Leaders, and contributes three Territories to a shared six-Territory battlefield called the Gauntlet.

    Players advance toward one another, fight battles, occupy and capture opposing Territories, develop Assets, and pursue faction-specific plans. The normal way to win is to cross the battlefield, capture the opponent's final Territory, force a Last Stand beyond the Gauntlet, and win that battle.

    01

    Components

    For a first game, use two prepared or recommended Decks. Each player needs one complete Deck, one Player Token, and a six-sided die. The two players' three-Territory sets join to form the six-position Gauntlet.

    A complete Deck includes a faction, one Leader from that faction, a Playable Deck, three different Territories, and any components required by that faction or Leader.

    02

    Cards, zones, and printed effects

    Keep every group of cards visibly separate. Your Hand is the permanent private set of cards you hold between turns. Your Draw Pile supplies cards; the Discard Pile is recyclable; the Graveyard normally is not. Assets remain face up in your Asset Bank.

    A battle creates three temporary areas: a Gambit set from Hand, a three-card Reserve drawn from the Draw Pile, and a Tactic chosen from that Reserve.

    • Action: normally play from Hand by taking 1 Action during Opening or Denouement.
    • Gambit: set from Hand during a battle.
    • Tactic: choose from Reserve during a battle.
    • Battle: may be used as either a Gambit or a Tactic.

    You normally take 1 Action each turn. Opening and Denouement are the two normal Action phases, before and after Movement. You normally take no more than 1 Action in either phase and no more than 1 Action total during the turn.

    03

    Setup

    Prepare the game in this order:

    1. Shuffle the Playable Deck and place it face down.
    2. Secretly arrange your three Territories in a line facing you.
    3. Join both players' Territory lines to form the six-position Gauntlet.
    4. Reveal all six Territories simultaneously.
    5. Prepare Leaders, trackers, references, and other faction components.
    6. Place each Player Token immediately before the Territory at that player's end.
    7. Each player draws three cards.
    8. Roll to determine the first player; reroll ties.

    Keep your Hand, temporary battle cards, Discard Pile, Graveyard, Assets, and faction components in clearly separate areas.

    04

    Your turn

    Complete each turn in this order:

    1. Capture: capture an opposing Territory you still occupy from the previous turn.
    2. Draw: draw one card.
    3. Opening: take your Action now, or save it for Denouement.
    4. Movement: advance, hold, or withdraw; resolve any battle immediately.
    5. Denouement: take your Action now if you did not take it during Opening.
    6. Cleanup: resolve end-of-turn effects and discard down to three cards in Hand.

    A battle ends the movement that started it. You move again afterward only when a rule or card specifically permits it.

    05

    Actions and Assets

    During Opening or Denouement, take 1 Action to play one card for its Action effect or use a Faction Feature marked 1 Action. Most one-time Action effects put their cards in the Discard Pile. Some cards become Assets, which stay in play and provide later effects.

    Your Asset limit equals the number of Territories you control. When banking a new Asset at the limit, you may discard one existing Asset to make room.

    06

    Movement and position

    During movement, you may move one position toward the opponent, remain where you are, or move one position toward your own end. Entering the opponent's position starts a battle.

    A battle stops the current movement. Winning does not automatically let you continue moving; follow-up movement must come from a card, Leader ability, or other specific effect.

    07

    Battles and the Aftermath

    A battle begins when one player enters the other's position. The moving player is the attacker and the other player is the defender.

    1. Begin Onset. Resolve Terms first when a Diplomat is involved, then resolve the remaining Onset effects.
    2. Each player may set one eligible card from Hand as a face-down Gambit.
    3. Set Hands physically aside and draw three cards to form each player's private Reserve.
    4. Reveal and resolve Gambits.
    5. Each player may choose one eligible Reserve card as a face-down Tactic.
    6. Reveal and resolve Tactics.
    7. Roll dice and determine the higher battle total.
    8. Resolve the Aftermath.

    If totals are tied while the defender controls the contested Territory, Defensive Edge means the defender wins the tie.

    During the Aftermath, the loser retreats and the winner takes or keeps the contested position. Gambits normally go to the Graveyard. Tactics and cards remaining in Reserve normally go to the Discard Pile.

    08

    Territory control and capture

    Your token shows where you are. A Territory's orientation shows who controls it. Winning a battle lets you occupy an opposing Territory, but you normally do not capture it immediately.

    You must still occupy that Territory at the start of your next turn. The current controller therefore normally receives one turn to drive you away before control changes.

    09

    Running the Gauntlet

    To win normally, push the opponent beyond their final Territory, capture that Territory, then advance beyond the column. This begins a Last Stand battle.

    The defender is harder to defeat there: Defensive Edge means they win tied battle totals, and they separately add +1 to their battle total. Win the Last Stand to run the Gauntlet and win the game.
