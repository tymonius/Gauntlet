## Front — Surveillance & Interference

### Faction Features

| Feature | Cost / Timing |
|---|---|
| Surveillance | No Action · 1 Intel per revealed card · Gambit and Tactic stages |
| Interference | No Action · 2 Intel per removed card · After reveal or face-up choice |

### Gambit Surveillance

After the opponent sets a face-down Gambit, spend **1 Intel** to reveal it. This opportunity is **once per battle**.

### Tactic Surveillance

After the opponent chooses face-down Tactics, spend **1 Intel per opposing Tactic** you reveal. This opportunity is **once per battle** and is separate from Gambit Surveillance.

### Interference

Immediately after revealing a card through Surveillance, spend **2 additional Intel per card** you remove.

- Removed Gambit → owner's **Hand**.
- Removed Tactic → owner's **Reserve**.
- The owner may choose one eligible replacement from the same source or pass.
- Replacements enter **face down**.

## Reverse — Mirrors & Replacements

### Replacement Window

After a replacement, continue the current stage without another Surveillance, Interference, reveal, or response window.

If you had already made your own choice at that stage, after the opponent replaces an interfered card you may revise your choice using the information already revealed.

### Direct Interference

If an opposing effect sets or chooses a card **face up**, spend **2 Intel** to Interfere at the same response timing.

Direct Interference spends that stage's Interference opportunity directly.

### Intelligence Mirror

At each choice stage:

1. First player makes the relevant choice.
2. Second player may use Surveillance / Interference, then makes their choice.
3. First player may use Surveillance / Interference, then revise their own choice if allowed.
4. The stage closes.

### Prevented Attempts

If an effect prevents Surveillance or Interference, the Intel remains spent and the relevant once-per-battle opportunity is still used.
