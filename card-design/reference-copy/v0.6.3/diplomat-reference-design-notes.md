# Diplomat reference design notes

The two faces are deliberately task-oriented rather than mirroring faction-guide organization.

- **Front — Terms:** Influence baseline, offering Terms, accepted Terms, refused Terms, and the Diplomat mirror rule.
- **Reverse — Outcomes & Treaties:** refused-Term outcomes, Leverage cost progression, Treaty Article reuse, Peace Treaty check, and the Action reminder.

Copy is intentionally shorter than the canonical rules and optimized for lookup during play. Full edge cases remain in the faction guide and on the relevant Proposal / Diplomat cards.
