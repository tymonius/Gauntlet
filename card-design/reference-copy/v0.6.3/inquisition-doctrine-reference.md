## Front — Conviction & Condemnation

### Faction Features

| Feature | Use |
|---|---|
| Conviction | Automatic · First qualifying Aftermath each turn |
| Condemnation | Automatic · Aftermath |
| Blasphemy | Automatic · Opposing Arcane Action / reveal |
| Purification | Automatic · Opponent's normal start-of-turn draw |

### Conviction

Begin at **0**. Maximum **4**.

The first time each turn one or more opposing cards enter the Graveyard during the Aftermath of a battle involving you, gain **1 Conviction**.

- This may occur during either player's turn.
- Winning is not required.
- Several qualifying cards from the same battle still give only 1 normal Conviction.

### Condemnation

During the Aftermath of battles involving you, opposing **Tactics go to their owner's Graveyard instead of their Discard Pile**.

Opposing Gambits already go to the Graveyard normally. Cards remaining in the opponent's Reserve are discarded normally.

## Reverse — Blasphemy & Purification

### Blasphemy

Gain **1 Conviction** whenever an opponent:

- plays an **Arcane** card for its Action effect; or
- reveals an Arcane Gambit or Tactic they control.

Blasphemy is outside the normal once-per-turn Conviction gain, but Conviction still cannot exceed 4.

Copying an Arcane card's effect does not count as playing or revealing that card unless an effect says otherwise.

### Purification

At the beginning of the opponent's turn, after their normal start-of-turn draw attempt, if they draw no cards because **both their Draw Pile and Discard Pile are empty**, you win immediately.

A failed Reserve draw or effect-generated draw does not trigger Purification.
