## Front — Rites & Progression

### Faction Features

| Feature | Use |
|---|---|
| Begin a Rite | 1 Action · Denouement |
| Invocation | No Action · Once per turn |
| Transmutation | No Action · Once per turn · Before dice |
| Begin Ritual | 1 Action · Denouement · After 3 Rites |
| Convergence | Automatic · During Ritual battle you initiated |

### Begin a Rite

During Denouement, spend **1 Action** to begin one incomplete Rite and pay its beginning cost.

- Only one Rite may be begun but incomplete at a time.
- You may begin the Rites in any order.
- A Rite cannot complete on the turn it begins.
- Complete at most one Rite per turn.
- If interrupted, reset it; paid costs are not returned unless stated.

### Progression

**1st Rite:** Unlock **Invocation**.

**2nd Rite:** Unlock **Transmutation**.

**3rd Rite:** Unlock **Convergence** and permission to begin the **Ritual of Ascension**.

### Invocation

Once per turn, after applying the Action, Gambit, Tactic, or Gambit/Tactic effect of an **Arcane** card you played, set, or chose, move **1 card from your Graveyard to your Discard Pile**.

### Transmutation

Once per turn, **before dice are rolled** in a battle involving you, put **1 card from Hand in your Graveyard** and add its value to your battle total.

The card is not played and none of its printed effects apply.

## Reverse — Ritual of Ascension

### Begin the Ritual

After completing all three Rites, during Denouement spend **1 Action** to bind:

- 1 Arcane card from your **Hand**;
- 1 Arcane card from your **Discard Pile**; and
- 1 Arcane card from your **Graveyard**.

### Convergence

While the Ritual is underway, during a battle **you initiated**, add **+1 to your battle total per card bound to the Ritual**.

With all three cards bound, Convergence normally gives **+3**.

### Complete & Win

Initiate a battle while all three Ritual cards remain bound. If you win that battle, complete the Ritual and **win immediately**.

### Interruption

If you lose any battle before Ritual completion, put all three Ritual-bound cards in your Graveyard. Completed Rites remain complete, and you may begin the Ritual again later.

**Withdrawal** neither completes nor interrupts the Ritual.

### Bound Cards

If a Rite or Ritual binding ends without another instruction, put its bound cards in their owners' Graveyards.
