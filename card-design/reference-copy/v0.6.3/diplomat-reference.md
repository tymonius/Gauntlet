## Front — Terms

### Influence

Start **1** · Maximum **10** · Staked Influence is unavailable until Terms conclude.

### Offer Terms

Pending battle · **before Onset** · normally one Proposal.

1. Choose an eligible Proposal.
2. Confirm its Requirement.
3. Confirm enough available Influence for its Stake.
4. Lower available Influence by the Stake.
5. Opponent **accepts** or **refuses**.

### Accepted

1. **No battle begins.** Resolve Accepted.
2. Return the Stake.
3. If unratified, flip to its Treaty Article side.
4. If newly ratified, gain **1 Influence**.
5. Resolve effects that occur after acceptance.

Unless the Proposal says otherwise: **attacker withdraws; defender remains.** No Onset, winner, loser, retreat, or Aftermath.

### Refused

1. Apply the Proposal's Refused effect and refusal modifiers.
2. Continue to **Gambits**.
3. Before dice, you may use **Leverage**.
4. Finish the battle through **Aftermath**.

### Diplomat Mirror

Attacker may offer first. If the attacker passes, defender may offer. Once either player offers Terms, the other cannot offer Terms for that battle.

## Reverse — Outcomes & Treaties

### Refused Result

| Result | Stake | Proposal | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Win | Return | If new: impose + flip | +2 Influence* |
| Lose | Lose | No ratification | — |
| No winner | Return | No ratification | — |

*Newly ratified only, unless stated otherwise. **Withdrawal creates no winner.**

### Leverage

After refused Terms, **before dice**: spend available Influence for a battle-total bonus. Staked Influence cannot be spent.

| Bonus | +1 | +2 | +3 | +4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Total cost | 1 | 3 | 6 | 10 |

The progression continues: each additional +1 costs **1 more Influence** than the previous increment.

### Treaty Articles

A ratified Proposal is a Treaty Article. It may be offered again, but cannot be ratified again and grants no normal newly-ratified Influence reward.

### Peace Treaty

**Check:** Start of your turn, after Capture and before Draw.

**Win:** Five different Proposals are ratified.

### Action Reminder

Offering Terms and using Leverage **do not take an Action**.
