Faction symbol review · Military

Approved cavalry-saber mark

Crossed cavalry sabers are now the production Military symbol. Their curved blades and protective guards give the faction a historically specific eighteenth-century command identity while remaining legible at the 0.16-inch Leader-header size. The earlier straight-sword alternatives remain below for comparison.
