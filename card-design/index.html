Diplomat Proposals / Treaty Articles

All 9 canonical v0.6.3 Proposals, in canonical order, each shown beside its ratified Treaty Article state. The unratified face uses the standard Diplomat card grammar with an artwork window; the ratified Treaty Article preserves the full Proposal text and Influence Stake while replacing the artwork with the formal ratification field and wax seal.

Mystics supplemental component set

Mystics Rites

All 3 double-sided Rites: Rite of Echoes, Rite of Blood, and Rite of Crossing. Each incomplete face uses the shared faction-component grammar with a Mystics artwork window and Rite instructions. The completed face replaces the illustration with an inked ritual diagram and turns the lower field into a progression reference for Invocation, Transmutation, Convergence, and the Ritual of Ascendance.
