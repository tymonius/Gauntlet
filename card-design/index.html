Design basis

What this pass changes

Adaptive composition

The framed illustration uses the largest approved height that the rules permit. Dense cards reduce art before reducing otherwise readable text.

Rules hierarchy

Action and Battle labels occupy a fixed left column while Adobe Caslon rules align in a wider right column. Reminders remain Caslon italic.

Header geometry

Titles remain all caps at one primary scale. The value medallion now shares the art frame’s right alignment and equal top inset.

Print and compare

Three density conditions

The same system now adapts its illustration height to each card. Rallying Cry also uses an existing Gauntlet painting so the frame, typography, crop, and finished artwork can be judged together.

Physical review

What to inspect after printing

  1. Whether each illustration reaches a satisfying size without forcing the rules below a comfortable reading threshold.
  2. Whether the finished command-tent painting integrates naturally with the ivory border, parchment field, and restrained frame.
  3. Whether all-caps P22 1722 gives the cards a stronger common silhouette without making long titles feel compressed.
  4. Whether Counterintelligence now reads at the same title scale as the other cards.
  5. Whether the Action and Battle columns align cleanly and remain easier to scan than a single full-width rules column.
  6. Whether the value medallion feels anchored by its shared alignment with the illustration frame.
