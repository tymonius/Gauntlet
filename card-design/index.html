Universal back color studies

Playable card back

The same production back is shown in all six faction palettes for comparison. Each study carries the faction hue through both the outer frame and the full inner field, with darker faction icons, the warm-ivory Gauntlet wordmark, and the pale-gold inset rule unchanged. These are colorway studies for selecting one universal back, not different backs for differently factioned cards.

Released base + candidate additions

All playable cards

The complete 128-card v0.6.3 release plus six proposed v0.6.4 faction additions, grouped by allegiance and rendered through the same production card path. Candidate cards remain outside canonical game data until their mechanics, costs, restrictions, and final Mystic title are locked.

Faction dashboards

All Leader cards

The complete twelve-Leader set, grouped by faction, with current abilities, resources, victory reminders, portraits, approved faction symbols, and metadata tints.

Diplomat components

Proposals / Treaty Articles

All 9 canonical Proposals in order, each beside its ratified Treaty Article state. Review both faces together without changing the production Diplomat card grammar.

Mystics components

Mystics Rites & Ritual

All 3 double-sided Rites—Echoes, Blood, and Crossing—plus the single-sided Ritual of Ascension victory card. The Rites show their incomplete and completed faces together; the Ritual uses its dedicated card back because completing it immediately wins the game.

Required faction components

Other supplemental cards

13 additional unique card designs account for 20 physical supplemental cards beyond Leaders, Proposals, Rites, and the Ritual. Finished renderers can replace these reserved production-size slots one component at a time without letting an unresolved card disappear from the design backlog.
