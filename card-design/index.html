Physical review

What to inspect after printing

  1. Whether the ivory border reads clearly in a fanned hand without consuming too much interior space.
  2. Whether P22 1722 remains legible and authoritative on both short and long titles.
  3. Whether the fixed illustration window leaves enough rules capacity on Counterintelligence.
  4. Whether the Caslon italic reminder is visibly secondary without becoming too faint or small.
  5. Whether the value medallion and footer feel like part of one document-like system rather than added interface widgets.
