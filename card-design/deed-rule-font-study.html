Deed ornamental-rule font study

This page searches the fonts actually loaded by the Gauntlet Adobe Fonts web project for Graveur Ornaments and LTC Fleurons, then renders every mapped ornament character beneath the Declaration Blackletter Deed wordmark.

No candidate on this page is applied to the production Deed. The goal is a single low, wide, symmetrical printer's rule: tapered or pointed ends, a compact geometric center, and substantially less visual weight than the title.

Target: something closer to a formal engraved divider than a calligraphic flourish — approximately 1–1.35 inches wide on the final 3.5-inch card, shallow in height, and visually quiet.

Adobe Fonts currently lists Graveur Ornaments with mappings on comma, digits, semicolon, A–Z, and a–z; LTC Fleurons lists mappings on ! # $ % & ( ) * 0–9 @ A–Z [ ] a–z ∏. The labels below preserve those source characters so any promising glyph can be reproduced exactly.
