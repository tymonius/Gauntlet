Deed horizontal ornamental-rule study

This study is intentionally different from the earlier fleuron atlas. It searches the Adobe kit for P22 Morris, P22 Arts & Crafts, and HWT Borders, then tests every source character as a long construction beneath the Declaration Blackletter Deed wordmark.

Each candidate shows the same glyph two ways: a seven-piece running band, and a mirrored left/right pair. The latter is meant to reveal half-rules or endpieces that look square in isolation but become a complete divider when reflected.

Target: handmade/historical rather than mechanically perfect; low and wide; roughly 5–8× wider than tall; compatible with blackletter; tapered or pointed outer ends; a restrained central join; and clearly subordinate to the title.
